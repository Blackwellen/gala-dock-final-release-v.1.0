---
title: Data Retention Schedule
summary: How long Gala Dock keeps each type of personal data, and the legal basis for each period.
---

We keep personal data only as long as the law requires or allows. At the end of each period, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised. If litigation, an investigation or a regulator requires it, we may keep specific records longer until the matter ends.

| Data | Kept for | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Account and profile | While the account is open; deleted or anonymised 14 days after you ask us to delete it (you can cancel in those 14 days), except data kept under the rows below | UK GDPR Art 5(1)(e); Art 17 |
| Orders, payments, refunds, chargebacks, payouts, invoices, ledger entries | 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to | Companies Act 2006 ss.386–388; VATA 1994 Sch 11 para 6 |
| Partner statements and self-billed invoices; self-billing agreements | 6 years after the end of the financial year | VAT Regulations 1995; VAT Notice 700/62 |
| Tax-reporting records about sellers (details collected, reports filed, reminders) | 6 years after the end of the reporting period | Platform Operators (Due Diligence and Reporting Requirements) Regulations 2023 |
| Bookings, quotes, contracts and saved terms | 6 years after the booking or event ends | Limitation Act 1980 s.5 |
| Tickets, transfers, resale records, check-ins, chargeback holds and voids | 6 years after the event | Limitation Act 1980 s.5; financial records |
| Disputes, chargebacks, support and trust and safety cases | 6 years after the case closes | Limitation Act 1980; Art 17(3)(e) |
| Complaints not linked to a dispute | 3 years after closure | Legitimate interests (service improvement, follow-up) |
| Online safety records (risk assessments, report and appeal records, statements of reasons) | Risk assessments: while in force + 3 years; reports and appeals: 3 years after closure, or 6 years if linked to a dispute or legal claim | Online Safety Act 2023 s.23; legal claims |
| Sanctions checks and decisions (refusals, overrides and their reasons) | 6 years after the decision | Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018; legal claims |
| Records of staff access to messages (break-glass grants, logs, audit entries) | 6 years after the grant ends, then deleted automatically | Limitation Act 1980 s.5; Art 5(2) |
| Other administrative audit logs | 6 years | Art 5(2); legal claims |
| Messages | While the conversation is in use. Messages you sent are removed when your account is deleted, unless needed for an open case or legal claim | Storage limitation; legal claims |
| Seller verification decisions and credential documents (such as licences and certificates) | While the listing is active and 6 years after. Identity document images are held by Stripe, not by us | Legal claims; consumer safety |
| Trader status declarations and acceptance records for legal terms (version, time, account) | 6 years after the account closes | Legal claims; Art 5(2) |
| Marketing consent records | While consent applies and 2 years after withdrawal | PECR; Art 7(1) |
| Security and sign-in logs; risk signals | 12 months (risk signals linked to a case: with the case) | Art 32 |
| Gala Agent requests and actions (organisers and suppliers) | 12 months, or longer when linked to a booking or approval kept under another row | Storage limitation; accountability |
| Encrypted backups of all the above | Removed from every backup within 65 days of deletion; erasures re-applied after any restore | Art 32; Art 5(1)(e) |
