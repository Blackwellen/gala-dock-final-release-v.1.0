title: Community and Review Standards
summary: The standards for honest listings, reliable conduct and genuine reviews on Gala Dock, what our badges mean, and how we enforce the standards.
---

## 1. Honest listings

1.1 Show what you actually offer. Use real photos; label stock or illustrative images.

1.2 Prices must include every compulsory charge; optional extras must be clearly optional.

1.3 Capacities must be safe and within licence.

1.4 Badges, awards and certifications you claim must be real and current.

1.5 **What Gala Dock's badges mean.** Each badge or label shows only what we checked, and when. For example: "Public liability insurance verified" means we reviewed a certificate showing public liability cover of at least £1,000,000 (or the higher minimum for the category in the Supplier Terms) valid on the date shown; a licence label means we reviewed the licence document named; a verified label on a review means the reviewer completed a paid booking or attended the event through Gala Dock. Badges are not a guarantee of quality, safety or compliance.

## 2. Reliable conduct

Respond within the stated window or decline promptly; honour quotes, holds and standby commitments; communicate through Gala Dock; turn up and deliver what was agreed. Your reliability score (response time, acceptance, on-time delivery, cancellations and disputes) is calculated automatically, explained in your workspace and affects search placement (Terms Schedule 1). Paid promotion is labelled "Sponsored" and never changes organic results.

## 3. Reviews

3.1 **Who can review.** Only customers with a completed booking or attended event, once per booking or event.

3.2 **Genuine only.** No fake, paid, incentivised or coordinated reviews; no reviewing yourself, connected businesses or competitors; no offering anything in return for a review or for changing one.

3.3 **How we protect review integrity.** We: allow reviews only from verified bookings and attendances; check for patterns that suggest fake reviews (for example related accounts, timing clusters); remove reviews we find to be fake and may restrict the accounts involved; never suppress, reorder or edit reviews to favour paying sellers; show all published reviews in averages; and show the date of each review.

3.4 **Responses.** Businesses may respond publicly once, politely, without personal data.

3.5 **Removal.** We remove reviews that are unlawful, abusive, irrelevant, contain personal data or are fake. We don't remove reviews just because they are negative. Each removal records a reason and can be appealed.

3.6 **If you think a review is defamatory.** Use **Report** on the review (choose "Something else" and explain) or email legal@galadock.com, giving your name, the review, what is untrue and why it harms you. We pass your complaint to the reviewer within 48 hours. If the reviewer doesn't reply within 5 days, or agrees, we remove it. If the reviewer stands by it and gives their details (or agrees we may share them), we tell you and leave it up. We follow the Defamation (Operators of Websites) Regulations 2013.

## 4. Respectful communication

No harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual content or pressure tactics in messages, listings or reviews. Report anything that worries you.

## 5. Templates and community content

Public templates must follow the Content and Template Policy. Templates with three or more independent reports are hidden automatically while we review them.

## 6. Enforcement ladder and reasons

1. Guidance.
2. Content action (editing a listing with notice; removing a review or template).
3. Restriction (publishing, messaging, resale, promotions, template publishing).
4. Suspension while we investigate a serious issue.
5. Termination for serious or repeated breaches, fraud or safety risks.

Serious harm can lead straight to suspension. We give a statement of reasons unless that would prejudice an investigation or the law prevents it. You can appeal through the Complaints Policy. Payouts follow the Payment Terms.
