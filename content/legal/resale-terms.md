---
title: Ticket Resale Terms
summary: How Gala Dock ticket resale works: when it is available, the price rule, how a sale and your refund work, and what buyers see before paying.
---

These terms add to the Ticket Terms (§7) for tickets resold through Gala Dock.

## 1. When resale is available

1.1 Only for events whose organiser has switched it on. Organisers can switch it off, set a lower cap or stop it at a set time before the event.

1.2 Not for designated football matches or where the law prohibits resale.

1.3 Only valid paid tickets bought or received through Gala Dock and held in your Gala Dock wallet. Tickets on hold because of a card dispute can't be listed.

## 2. Price

2.1 **You sell at the price you paid.** If that is above the organiser's resale cap (a percentage of face value), the ticket can't be listed. We enforce this automatically.

2.2 Buyers pay a £1.00 booking fee, included in the total shown from the first price display.

## 3. How a sale works

3.1 Your ticket stays yours until someone buys it. You can withdraw the listing until a buyer starts checkout.

3.2 When a buyer pays, the **organiser sells the buyer a new ticket** for the same seat or area, with a new code; your ticket is cancelled (so screenshots or printouts no longer work).

3.3 **Your money:** the organiser refunds you the price you paid to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 working days. Your original £1.00 booking fee isn't refunded.

## 4. What buyers see before paying

The event, date and venue; the ticket type and the seat, row or standing area; the original face value; any restrictions on use (age, ID, entry conditions); a ticket reference; and that the ticket is **sold by the event organiser, named at checkout, through Gala Dock resale**, replacing a ticket returned by another fan.

## 5. Rules for sellers

You must not: use bots or automated tools to buy or list; list tickets you don't hold or aren't entitled to sell; offer the same ticket elsewhere at the same time; or resell as a business without telling us. We may cancel listings and restrict accounts that break these rules, and report suspected offences.

## 6. If the event changes or is cancelled

The resale buyer gets the refund the Ticket Terms describe. Your refund for the resale stands.

## 7. Responsibility

The organiser is responsible for the event and for refunds of its ticket sales. We run resale with reasonable care and skill and issue valid replacement tickets. Your statutory rights aren't affected.
