2.1 You must be 18 or over to create an account, buy tickets or make bookings. Some events have higher age limits; you may be asked for ID.

## 3. Prices and what you pay

3.1 The price you see first includes every compulsory charge, including our fees and VAT. Optional extras are shown separately and added only if you choose them.

3.2 **Tickets:** the organiser sets the face value. Gala Dock charges a booking fee of £1.00 per paid ticket for our booking service.

3.3 **Bookings:** the seller sets its prices and may ask for a deposit and a later balance. The quote shows the deposit, balance, due dates and cancellation policy before you accept. Gala Dock charges a booking admin fee of 2.5% of each payment (minimum £1), refunded if the seller cancels.

3.4 **If a deposit is taken more than 58 days before your event**, the seller may be paid it before the event. The seller remains responsible for refunding you if the booking doesn't go ahead.

3.5 Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see your full card number. A payment is confirmed only when Stripe confirms it; we never take a payment twice for the same order.

## 4. Requests, quotes, holds and bookings

4.1 A quote is an offer by the seller. **A booking is made when you accept the quote and pay any deposit** (or the seller confirms, where no deposit is needed). The booking includes the Standard Booking Terms.

4.2 Holds and standby arrangements are explained on screen with their expiry time.

4.3 Give accurate event details: date, times, guest numbers, access, dietary requirements and allergens. If you change them after booking, the seller may need to re-quote.

4.4 **Private sellers.** If a seller is shown as a private individual, not a business, some consumer law rights (for example under the Consumer Rights Act 2015) may not apply to your contract with them.

4.5 **Enquiry-only sellers.** Some sellers can only take enquiries through Gala Dock. If you agree to pay them directly, Gala Dock's payment rules, disputes process and refunds don't apply.

## 5. Your rights to cancel

5.1 **Tickets, and venue hire, catering and other services related to leisure activities for a specific date**, are exempt from the usual 14-day right to cancel online purchases (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regulation 28(1)(b)). Your cancellation rights for those are set by the seller's cancellation policy shown before you pay, the Standard Booking Terms and the Cancellation and Refund Policy.

5.2 **Other services** booked online that aren't for a specific date (for example design work or goods): you have 14 days to cancel. If you ask the seller to start within those 14 days, you'll pay for what has been provided up to cancellation, and you lose the right once the service is fully provided. We'll ask you to confirm both points with separate ticks.

5.3 If the **organiser cancels** an event or the **seller cancels** your booking, you get a full refund of what you paid for it, including our fee.

## 6. Payments and problems with a booking

6.1 When you pay a seller, the money waits in the seller's own Stripe account and isn't paid out to the seller until the booking is delivered (you confirm it, or 24 hours after the event with no dispute opened), the parties settle, or the Payment Terms otherwise allow. This gives you time to raise a problem.

6.2 If something goes wrong, message the seller first. If you can't resolve it, open a **dispute** from the booking **within 24 hours after the event ends**. That time limit only affects when the seller is paid; you keep all your legal rights, including going to court and asking your card issuer for a chargeback.

6.3 We may give a recommendation based on the booking record, messages, photos and other evidence, but we don't decide disputes. You're never required to use arbitration for claims of £5,000 or less. See the Disputes Policy.
