## 1. Who these terms apply to

They apply when you buy a Gala Dock plan or AI credit pack. Gala Dock (Blackwellen Limited, company 16482166, VAT GB508480488, 61 Bridge Street, Kington, Herefordshire, HR5 3DJ) is the seller and merchant of record. Plans and credit packs are sold **only to businesses** (organisers, suppliers, venues and partners acting for their business), not to consumers. Before your first purchase you give your **business name** (and VAT number, if you have one); we show them on our VAT invoices, and by buying you confirm you are buying for your business.

## 2. Plans

2.1 Plans (Starter, Pro, Business, Enterprise) give software features and limits: active events (drafts and archived events don't count), team seats (owner, active members and pending invites), published listings, storage, AI credits, API access and advanced reporting, as shown on the pricing page at purchase.

2.2 **Limits** apply when you create or publish something new. Reaching a limit never deletes or unpublishes what you already have; you'll be offered an upgrade.

2.3 Plans don't buy bookings, work or organic search ranking. Plan level sets the commission rate on bookings (Supplier Terms 5.3).

## 3. Price, billing and VAT

3.1 Prices for business users are shown excluding VAT. VAT is added at the rate that applies to you (UK VAT for UK customers; reverse charge for business customers elsewhere who give a valid tax number; local VAT or GST where the law requires) and shown on your invoice.

3.2 Plans are billed in advance, monthly or yearly, to your saved payment method, and **renew automatically** for the same period until cancelled. Your renewal date is shown in Settings → Payments & Billing.

3.3 Changing interval or upgrading takes effect at once, with a prorated charge or credit. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current period; you can undo a scheduled downgrade before then.

3.4 If a payment fails, Stripe retries it and the plan shows as past due in Settings → Payments & Billing, where you can update your payment method. If the payment still can't be collected, the subscription ends and the workspace moves to the free Starter plan (limits apply as in 2.2).

## 4. Cancelling

4.1 Cancel any time in Settings → Payments & Billing (Stripe customer portal). Your plan stays active to the end of the paid period.

4.2 We don't refund part-periods unless the law requires it or we failed to provide the service.

## 5. AI credits

5.1 Plans include a monthly allowance of AI credits for Gala Agent (organisers and suppliers only); you can buy credit packs.

5.2 Each calendar month's included allowance is used first and doesn't roll over to the next month. Purchased credits are used next, oldest first, and expire **12 months** after purchase unless the pack says otherwise when you buy it.

5.3 Credits wrongly used because of our fault are restored. A refunded credit pack removes its unused credits.

5.4 The credit cost of each quality mode is shown in the app; we won't change it without notice.

## 6. Partners

If you were referred by a partner, the partner may earn commission on what you pay. Partners see totals only, never your workspace name.

## 7. Changes

We give at least 30 days' notice of price changes, before your next renewal. You can cancel before the change applies.
