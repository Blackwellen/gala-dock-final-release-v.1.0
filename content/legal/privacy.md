This policy explains how Blackwellen Limited, trading as Gala Dock ("we"), collects and uses personal data when you use galadock.com, our apps and services as a customer, organiser, venue or supplier, team member, promoter or partner. We follow the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR).

## 2. The data we collect

- **Account and profile:** name, email, phone, password (only as a secure hash), photo, home area, language, interests, notification and marketing preferences, two-step verification settings.
- **Events and planning:** events you create or join, guest lists, plans, budgets, tasks, programmes, seating and venue layouts.
- **Marketplace activity:** enquiries, quotes, holds, bookings, cancellations, reviews and disputes.
- **Tickets:** orders, ticket-holder and attendee names, seats, transfers, resale listings, check-ins, and whether a ticket is on hold because of a card dispute.
- **Payments:** amounts, status, refunds, chargebacks and payouts. Card details are collected by Stripe; we never see your full card number.
- **Sellers and workspaces:** business details and business billing details, trader status declaration, listings, team members, credential documents (such as licences and certificates), payout and verification status from Stripe Connect (we receive the result of Stripe's identity checks, not ID images), and **tax-reporting details** (name, address, date of birth, tax identification number, VAT number, amounts earned).
- **Partners and promoters:** application details, VAT status and number, attributed referrals (totals), commission statements.
- **Messages** between members, workspaces and support.
- **Support, safety and risk:** support cases, reports, trust and safety cases, and fraud and risk signals.
- **Technical:** device and browser information, IP address and the country it indicates, sign-in events, security logs, Cloudflare Turnstile checks, and the cookies and storage in our Cookie Policy.
- **Legal acceptances:** which version of each legal document you accepted, and when.
- **Gala Agent (organisers and suppliers only):** instructions you give and actions it prepares or takes. Gala Agent isn't available in customer accounts.

Special category data: you may tell us about dietary needs (which can reveal religion or health) or access needs (health). We use it only to deliver your booking or ticket, on the basis of your explicit consent or because you made it available for that purpose, and an organiser or supplier who collects it must have its own lawful basis.

## 3. When an organiser or supplier is the controller

- **Attendee and guest data an organiser collects** for its event: the organiser is the controller and we are its processor under our Data Processing Addendum.
- **Your booking details given to a supplier or venue** so it can deliver: the supplier and we are each independent controllers.

Questions about those uses should go to the organiser or supplier first; we'll help them respond.

- **A buyer of our business**, subject to this policy.

We don't sell your personal data.

### Calendar integrations

If you choose to connect a Google or Microsoft 365 calendar, we use it only to keep your schedule in sync. You can disconnect at any time, or use a calendar subscription link instead.

- **What we write:** a separate calendar called "Gala Dock" in your account, containing your Gala Dock events and tickets (name, time and location). We don't change your other calendars.
- **What we read:** only when you're busy (start and end times), so we can show your availability. We don't read the titles, attendees, descriptions or contents of your other events.
- **What we store:** the email address of the connected account, your busy time ranges, and the access tokens. The tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and can't be read by any part of the app you or other members use.
- **Permissions requested:** Google: access to calendars the app creates (`calendar.app.created`) and free/busy information (`calendar.freebusy`). Microsoft: `Calendars.ReadWrite`.
- **Retention:** deleted when you disconnect the calendar or delete your Gala Dock account.

Gala Dock's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide calendar sync, we don't sell it or use it for advertising, we don't use it to train AI models, and we don't share it with anyone except as needed to provide the sync, to comply with the law, or with your permission. People at Gala Dock don't read it unless you ask us to, for security, or where the law requires.

## 7. Access to your messages by Gala Dock staff

Your conversations are private, and our staff cannot browse them. In limited cases authorised staff may review specific conversations linked to an open support or trust and safety case: to protect someone's safety; to investigate fraud or abuse; to resolve a payment dispute; to respond to a legal request; or for a critical support issue.

When this happens:

- access is read-only and time-limited;
- it requires a recorded reason and fresh identity verification;
- high-risk access needs approval from a second administrator where one is available;
- every conversation opened is logged and reviewed by our data protection lead;
- staff can never send, edit or delete messages as you.

Where it is lawful and would not prejudice a fraud, safety or legal investigation, we tell you afterwards that a review took place.

## 8. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for these purposes or the law; then we delete or anonymise it. Periods are in the [Data Retention Schedule](/data-retention) (for example financial and tax-reporting records 6 years; staff access records 6 years).

**Backups.** Supabase keeps daily backups for 7 days. Our own encrypted copies are kept for up to 63 days (hourly and daily copies up to 8 days, weekly up to 29 days, monthly up to 63 days). Deleted data leaves every backup within 65 days. If we ever restore a backup, we re-apply earlier deletion and erasure requests before the data is used.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to its processing (including to marketing at any time), receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. Some rights are limited where we must keep data by law or for legal claims. Contact legal@galadock.com. We reply within one month, or tell you if we need up to two more months.

## 10. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest; database access controls that limit each account to its own data; two-step verification for staff administrators with fresh verification for sensitive actions; malware scanning of uploads; audit logging; regular access reviews. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

## 11. Children

Accounts are for people aged 18 or over. Tickets may be bought for younger attendees where the organiser allows; the adult buyer is responsible for the attendee information they give. We don't knowingly collect data from children except attendee names and any details an adult provides for them.

## 12. Complaints

Please contact our data protection lead first at legal@galadock.com; we'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you the outcome. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/), 0303 123 1113.
