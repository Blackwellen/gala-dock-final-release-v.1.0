title: Complaints Policy
summary: How to complain about Gala Dock itself, including moderation and enforcement decisions, online safety and data protection, and what happens next.

We want to put things right when something goes wrong. This policy covers complaints about Gala Dock itself: our platform, staff, fees, moderation and enforcement decisions, online safety, and data protection.

**Complaints about an event or a seller's service** go first to the organiser or seller through Gala Dock messaging, then to a dispute from the booking (Disputes Policy). Gala Dock recommends but doesn't decide those disputes, so there is no appeal to us against a recommendation; you can escalate to an independent body or court.

## 1. How to complain

In the app: Help → Contact support → Complaint · Email: support@galadock.com (subject "Complaint") · Post: Blackwellen Limited, 61 Bridge Street, Kington, Herefordshire, HR5 3DJ. Tell us what happened, when, the reference, and what you'd like us to do.

## 2. What happens next

| Stage | Timescale |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Within 2 working days |
| Investigation and response | Within 10 working days; complex cases within 28 days, and we'll tell you if longer and why |
| Review | If you're unhappy, ask within 28 days. A senior person not involved before reviews it and responds within 15 working days. While only one person can take these decisions, an independent reviewer we appoint does the review |

## 3. Appeals against moderation and enforcement

If we removed content, restricted or suspended your account, hid a listing or template, or decided not to act on your report, you can appeal through this process. The reviewer looks at the original decision and new information, decides to uphold, change or reverse it, and gives written reasons.

## 4. Data protection complaints

Complain to our data protection lead at legal@galadock.com; we acknowledge within 30 days. You can also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).

## 5. Online safety complaints

You can complain about: illegal content you reported; a decision to remove your content or restrict your account because we considered it illegal or in breach of our terms; or our compliance with our duties under the Online Safety Act 2023 (for example, how our reporting works). These follow §2 and §3. You can also contact Ofcom.

## 6. Business users

Organisers, suppliers, venues and partners can complain about restrictions, suspensions, ranking or any other matter through this process. We consider each complaint, deal with it within the timescales above and tell you the outcome.

## 7. Alternative dispute resolution and courts

If we can't resolve a consumer complaint, we'll tell you in writing. We aren't currently signed up to an ADR scheme. You can go to court. Card payments may be protected by your card issuer's chargeback scheme or section 75 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974.

## 8. Records

Complaint records are kept for 3 years after closure, or 6 years if linked to a dispute or legal claim (Data Retention Schedule).
