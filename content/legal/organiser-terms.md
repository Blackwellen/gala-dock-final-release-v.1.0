1.1 **Independent business.** You run your events in your own name and at your own risk. You aren't our employee, worker, partner, joint venturer, agent or contractor, and can't bind us.

1.2 **Roles and security.** You control who is in your workspace and their roles. You may require two-step verification. You are responsible for activity under your credentials and API keys until you tell us they are compromised and we've had a reasonable time to act.

1.3 **Plan limits.** Your plan sets limits (active events, team seats including pending invites, storage, AI credits, API, advanced reporting). Limits apply when you create or publish something new; they never delete existing data.

1.4 **Trader status.** You confirm whether you sell as a business. If you sell as a private individual, buyers are told, and some consumer rights against you may not apply; you must not mislead buyers about this.

## 2. Selling tickets

2.1 **You are the seller and merchant of record.** You sell tickets in your own name. Each ticket contract is between you and the buyer. Buyers pay directly into your own Stripe account. Gala Dock doesn't buy or sell your tickets; it acts for you only to let buyers conclude contracts **in your name** on Gala Dock's checkout, to issue the tickets you sell, and to send Stripe payout and refund instructions under the Payment Terms. Your name and contact details are shown to buyers.

2.2 **Your obligations to attendees.** You must: describe the event accurately (date, time, venue, line-up, age limits, accessibility, restrictions); publish event conditions and your refund approach before sale; run the event as advertised with reasonable care and skill; and hold every licence, permission and insurance it needs (premises licence or temporary event notice, music licensing, safety certificates, capacity limits, and the duties of the Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025 once they apply).

2.3 **Pricing.** You set face values. Buyers see the total, including Gala Dock's £1.00 booking fee, from the first price shown.

2.4 **Cancelling an event.** You must cancel through Gala Dock. Cancelling voids all tickets and refunds every buyer in full, including the booking fee, from your Stripe balance, whatever the reason. You can't cancel an event with paid orders any other way.

2.5 **Postponing or changing.** Buyers keep their tickets. Where the change is material, you must let buyers ask for a refund for at least 14 days after they are told.

2.6 **Resale and transfers.** You decide whether tickets can be transferred or resold through Gala Dock and may set a resale cap at or below face value. A resale is your sale of a new ticket to the new buyer, with a refund of the original buyer's price from your balance. Resale isn't available for designated football matches.

2.7 **Check-in.** You're responsible for door staff and ID checks. You must follow the scanner result ("already used", "wrong event", "on hold") unless you have a good reason to override it; overrides are logged and at your risk.

2.8 **Chargebacks on tickets.** If a buyer disputes a ticket payment, the tickets in that order are put on hold; they are restored if you win and cancelled (with capacity returned) if the buyer wins (Ticket Terms §8). You bear the chargeback and Stripe's dispute fee (Payment Terms 6). If you admit a held ticket, you do so at your own risk.

## 3. Buying from venues and suppliers

3.1 Payments, disputes and cancellations follow the Payment Terms, the Disputes Policy, the Standard Booking Terms and the seller's saved cancellation policy.

3.2 You must give suppliers accurate information (dates, times, access, power, guest numbers, dietary and allergen needs, safety requirements) and pay on the agreed schedule.

3.3 **Keeping bookings on Gala Dock.** For 12 months after your first real contact with a supplier or venue through Gala Dock (a message, request, quote or booking), you must not arrange to pay them outside Gala Dock for an engagement that started on Gala Dock, unless they are enquiry-only (Terms §4A) or we agree in writing. If you do, we may charge a fee equal to the commission that would have been payable on the off-platform payments, once only, after telling you what we found and giving you 14 days to respond; and off-platform payments aren't covered by the Payment Terms.

## 4. Fees

4.1 **Ticket fees.** For each paid ticket: Gala Dock's booking fee of **£1.00** (including VAT), paid by the buyer; and a seller fee of **5% of face value**, plus VAT where it applies, deducted by Stripe from the payment as Gala Dock's application fee. Free tickets carry no fees. Resale carries the buyer booking fee only.

4.2 **Buyer fees are Gala Dock's.** You collect the booking fee as Gala Dock's agent within the buyer's payment; it isn't your income (Payment Terms 2A).

4.3 **Fees when you cancel** (express term, confirmed with its own tick). By the time you cancel, we have provided our services for the tickets sold: listing, checkout, payment processing, fraud screening, ticket issue and refund processing. So, unless 4.4 or 4.5 applies, you pay: (a) the 5% seller fee on tickets sold; and (b) an amount equal to the £1.00 booking fees refunded to buyers. These are charges for services already performed, not a penalty, and never more than the fees in 4.1 for the tickets sold. We show the amount before you confirm the cancellation; it is invoiced, payable within 14 days.

4.4 **Force majeure: fees waived.** We waive the amounts in 4.3 if the event can't lawfully or safely take place because of an event outside your reasonable control that you couldn't reasonably have avoided, for example: an order of a government, police or licensing authority; the venue closed or made unsafe by fire, flood, structural or utility failure; a Met Office red or amber warning or declared emergency at the location; war, terrorism or civil unrest; a national period of mourning requiring cancellation. It **doesn't** include: low sales, higher costs or commercial reasons; a performer, supplier or venue cancelling, unless itself caused by force majeure; failing to obtain licences, permissions or insurance you could reasonably have obtained; circumstances you knew or should have expected when you put tickets on sale; or where the event could reasonably have gone ahead on the same date in a modified form or at a suitable alternative venue.

4.5 **Gala Dock cancellations.** No fees are payable if we cancel or suspend your event, unless because of your breach of these terms or the law.

4.6 **Force-majeure claims.** Choose "Force majeure" when you cancel and give evidence. Fees are on hold while we review. We aim to decide within 5 working days with reasons. You may send further evidence within 14 days and we'll look again. This doesn't affect any other rights.

4.7 **Bookings you make.** You pay a booking admin fee of 2.5% of each payment (minimum £1), refunded if the seller cancels.

4.8 Fee changes need at least 30 days' notice and never apply to tickets already sold.

## 5. When you are paid

5.1 Ticket, resale and merchandise money stays in your own Stripe balance and is paid out **5 UK business days after the event ends**, less fees, refunds, chargebacks and any reserve, under the Payment Terms. It is never paid out before the event. Paid tickets can go on sale only from the date shown in Ticket setup.

5.2 Payouts are paused only on the grounds in Payment Terms 9.
