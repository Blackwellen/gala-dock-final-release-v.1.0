---
title: Acceptable Use and Online Safety Policy
summary: What everyone using Gala Dock must not do, how to report content or behaviour, how we decide, and how to appeal.
---

This policy applies to everyone who uses Gala Dock (customers, organisers, suppliers, venues, partners, promoters, and anyone using our API, MCP server or Gala Agent). Breaking it can lead to content removal, restrictions, suspension, termination and reports to the police or other authorities.

## 1. Illegal and harmful content and activity

You must not use Gala Dock to post, share or do anything illegal, including:

- **fraud:** fake events, venues, suppliers or tickets; taking payment for services you won't provide; asking someone to pay outside Gala Dock so as to avoid its payment rules (except enquiry-only sellers, §2); phishing; identity misuse;
- threats, harassment, stalking, bullying, or abuse, including on grounds of a protected characteristic; content stirring up hatred;
- sharing or threatening to share intimate images without consent;
- child sexual exploitation and abuse in any form, and any attempt to contact children for sexual purposes;
- terrorism content;
- selling or promoting illegal goods or services, weapons, drugs, or anything needing a licence you don't hold;
- events that are unlawful, unlicensed where a licence is needed, or unsafe (for example deliberately exceeding safe capacity);
- money laundering, terrorist financing, or breaching sanctions;
- facilitating unlawful work or human trafficking (for example staffing listings that exploit workers).

## 2. Marketplace integrity

You must not: post false, misleading or bait-and-switch listings, prices or availability; hide compulsory charges or add surprise fees; write fake reviews, review your own business or competitors, offer incentives for reviews, or suppress honest reviews; manipulate search, rankings, reliability scores or promotions; create multiple accounts to avoid restrictions, limits or fees; or, as a business user, take payment outside Gala Dock for an engagement that started on Gala Dock (Terms §4.8). **Enquiry-only sellers** may agree payment directly but must say clearly that Gala Dock's payment rules and protection don't apply.

## 3. Tickets

No bots, scripts or tools to buy tickets or get around limits or queues (this may be a criminal offence); no reselling above the event's resale rules or where resale is prohibited; no copying, forging or sharing credentials for unauthorised entry.

## 4. Security and technical misuse

No unauthorised access or attempts; no probing or vulnerability testing except under responsible disclosure (report security issues to support@galadock.com); no bypassing security features, rate limits, permissions, maintenance controls, AI credit limits or Agent approvals, including by prompt injection; no malware; no overloading or scraping beyond our API and robots rules; no sharing API keys or using another workspace's keys; no sending data through integrations to unauthorised destinations.

## 5. Communications

Messaging is for genuine event, booking and planning communication. No spam, unsolicited marketing, phishing, or collecting personal data for unrelated purposes. Marketing to attendees must follow PECR and UK GDPR.

## 6. Content rights

Don't upload content you don't have the rights to use. Follow the Content and Template Policy.

## 7. Enforcement

We act proportionately: warning, content removal, feature restrictions (publishing, messaging, resale, promotions), suspension or termination. Serious harm can lead straight to suspension. **Money already in a seller's Stripe account follows the Payment Terms; payouts are paused only on the grounds in Payment Terms §9.**

## 8. Reporting

8.1 Signed-in users can report content or behaviour with **Report** on the message, business profile, listing, event page, review or template. Anyone, with or without an account, can report by email to support@galadock.com. Emergencies: call 999. Safeguarding concerns: see the Safeguarding Policy.

8.2 Tell us what the content is, where it is and why you think it is illegal or breaks this policy.

8.3 We acknowledge reports and aim to review: risks to life or children immediately; fraud and threats within 24 hours; other reports within 5 working days.

## 9. How we decide

9.1 A trained person reviews reports and automated signals before we act against an account, except that a file failing a malware scan is never made available.

9.2 Tools we use: malware scanning of uploads; automated fraud and abuse signals; and rate limits.

9.3 We take account of freedom of expression and privacy, especially for honest negative reviews.

## 10. Statements of reasons

We tell the affected user what we decided, which rule it breached, what we did and how to appeal, and we tell the reporter the outcome, unless the law forbids it or it would put someone at risk.

## 11. Appeals and complaints

11.1 You can appeal a decision (removal, restriction, suspension, or a decision not to act on your report) under the Complaints Policy within 28 days. Someone not involved in the decision reviews it; while only one member of our team takes these decisions, an independent reviewer we appoint decides appeals.

11.2 You can also complain about how we've handled our online safety duties. You may contact Ofcom, the ICO or the courts.
