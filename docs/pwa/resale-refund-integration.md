# Resale proceeds: original payment method

The user explicitly chose refunds to the original payment method. A seller must be the paying buyer recorded on this ticket's purchase order. A recipient of a transferred ticket cannot refund another person's purchase. A buyer who subsequently purchases a resale ticket has their own purchase order and can qualify against that payment.

Resale remains at the ticket price paid. The original booking fee is excluded from seller proceeds. The new buyer's fee is calculated by checkout. Only GBP purchases with an issued, non-refunding order and a recorded Stripe Checkout session are eligible in the current GBP checkout architecture.

`202610280026_resale_original_payment_refunds.sql` was applied to the linked Supabase project. Ticket-state and reservation triggers enforce the original payer at the database boundary, including existing listings. New payout records persist their source purchase. Service-only leased claims serialize partial refunds of a purchase; cancellation claims wait for processing or failed resale jobs to be reconciled. Ticket issuance locks the event so it cannot race cancellation and create an active ticket afterwards.

The payments cron invokes `processResaleRefunds` under its existing bearer authentication and payment maintenance guard. The worker verifies the recorded Checkout session, order reference, currency and total; rejects a disputed or insufficient original charge; persists request time and provider refund ID; and only records `paid` after Stripe reports `succeeded`. Pending/requires-action refunds are polled. Lost responses are recovered by payout metadata. An unresolved request older than 23 hours requires reconciliation rather than reusing an expired key and risking a duplicate. Stripe supports partial original-method refunds and may prune idempotency keys after 24 hours. [Refund API](https://docs.stripe.com/api/refunds/create), [refund listing](https://docs.stripe.com/api/refunds/list), [idempotency](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests).

Cancellation refunds return the remaining purchase balance after completed resale refunds. When the entire purchase is already returned, completion requires successful provider refund records covering the charge; a pending refund is insufficient. The charge-refunded webhook now posts only the new cumulative delta, with atomic replay/out-of-order protection and immutable correction of legacy cumulative overstatements.
