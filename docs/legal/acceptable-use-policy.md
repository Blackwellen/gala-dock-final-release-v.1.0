# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Approved for publication by Jamahl Thomas, Director, Blackwellen Limited, 12 October 2026.** Version 2026-10.

This policy applies to everyone who uses Gala Dock: customers, organisers, providers, partners, promoters, and anyone using our API, MCP server or Gala Agent. Breaking it can lead to content removal, account restrictions, suspension, termination and, where appropriate, reports to the police or other authorities.

## 1. Illegal and harmful activity

You must not use Gala Dock to:
* organise, promote or sell tickets to events that are unlawful, unlicensed where a licence is required, or unsafe (for example deliberately exceeding safe capacity);
* sell or promote illegal goods or services, weapons, drugs or anything that requires a licence you don't hold;
* commit or facilitate fraud, money laundering, terrorist financing or sanctions breaches;
* exploit, endanger or harm children or vulnerable people (see the Safeguarding & Abuse Policy);
* harass, threaten, stalk, bully or discriminate against anyone, including on the grounds of a protected characteristic;
* share intimate images without consent, or content that incites violence or hatred.

## 2. Marketplace integrity

You must not:
* post false, misleading or bait-and-switch listings, prices or availability;
* hide compulsory charges or add surprise fees after booking;
* write fake reviews, review your own business, offer incentives for reviews, or suppress honest reviews;
* ask for or accept payment outside Gala Dock for engagements that started on Gala Dock;
* manipulate search, rankings, reliability scores or promotions, for example with fake accounts or bookings;
* create multiple accounts to avoid restrictions, limits or fees.

## 3. Tickets

You must not:
* use bots, scripts or automated tools to buy tickets, or to get around purchase limits or queues (this may be a criminal offence);
* sell Gala Dock tickets above the price allowed by the event's resale rules, or resell where resale is prohibited;
* copy, forge or share ticket credentials for unauthorised entry.

## 4. Security and technical misuse

You must not:
* access accounts, data or systems without authorisation, or try to;
* probe, scan or test vulnerabilities, except under our responsible disclosure process (support@galadock.com);
* bypass or interfere with security features, rate limits, permissions, maintenance controls, AI credit limits or Agent approval steps, including through prompt injection;
* upload malware or content designed to exploit software;
* overload the service, or scrape it beyond what our API and robots rules permit;
* share API keys publicly or use another workspace's keys;
* use webhooks or integrations to send data to destinations you aren't authorised to use.

## 5. Communications

Messaging is for genuine event, booking and planning communication. No spam, unsolicited marketing, phishing, or collecting personal data for purposes unrelated to the booking. Marketing to attendees must comply with PECR and UK GDPR.

## 6. Content

Don't upload content you don't have the rights to use. Follow the Content & Template Policy.

## 7. Enforcement

We act proportionately: warnings, content removal, feature restrictions (for example publishing, messaging, resale or payouts), suspension, or termination. Where safe and lawful, we explain the decision and how to appeal (see the Complaints Policy). Funds held for someone whose account is restricted are handled under the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms and the law.

## 8. Reporting

Report content or behaviour using the Report option in the app, or email support@galadock.com. Safeguarding concerns: see the Safeguarding & Abuse Policy. Emergencies: call 999.
