# Official Ticket Resale Terms

> **Approved for publication by Jamahl Thomas, Director, Blackwellen Limited, 12 October 2026.** Version 2026-10. **Resale law must be re-checked immediately before release**, including any secondary-ticketing price-cap legislation.

Gala Dock's official resale lets ticket holders who can't attend sell their tickets to other fans, safely and at a fair price. These terms apply to sellers and buyers using resale and sit alongside the Ticket Purchase Terms.

## 1. When resale is available

1.1 Resale is available only for events whose **organiser has enabled it**. Organisers can switch it off, set a lower price cap, or stop it at a set time before the event.

1.2 We don't allow resale of tickets for designated football matches or any other event where resale is prohibited by law.

1.3 Only tickets bought or received through Gala Dock, held in your Gala Dock wallet and still valid can be listed.

## 2. Price

2.1 **You can't sell a ticket for more than you paid for it.** The listing price is the lower of the price you paid and the organiser's resale cap (a percentage of face value). We enforce this automatically.

2.2 Buyers may pay a booking fee, shown before they pay, as for any ticket.

## 3. What happens when you sell

3.1 When you list a ticket, it remains yours until someone buys it. You can withdraw the listing until a buyer starts checkout.

3.2 When a buyer pays, **your ticket is cancelled and a brand-new ticket is issued to the buyer** with a new code and QR credential, so your old ticket (including any screenshot or printout) no longer works.

3.3 Your sale proceeds are recorded as a payout to you. We pay them after the payment clears, using the payout method in your account. [Counsel/finance: confirm the payout timing — the implementation records a pending payout and does not move money until payouts are processed.]

## 4. Information shown to buyers

Before buying, the buyer sees: the event, date and venue; the ticket type, and seat, row or area where applicable; the face value; any restrictions on use (age, ID, entry conditions); and the fact that the ticket is sold through official resale by another fan. We don't reveal the seller's identity unless the law requires it. Where it does, we disclose what is required.

## 5. Rules for sellers

You must not:
* use bots or automated tools to buy or list tickets;
* list tickets you don't hold or aren't entitled to sell;
* sell, or advertise for sale, the same ticket anywhere else at the same time;
* run resale as a business without telling us. Traders must identify themselves as traders.

We may cancel listings and restrict accounts that break these rules, and we report suspected offences where appropriate.

## 6. If the event changes or is cancelled

If an event is cancelled after a resale, **the buyer** gets the refund described in the Cancellation & Refund Policy. The seller's sale stands as completed unless the organiser's refund process says otherwise.

## 7. Liability

The organiser is responsible for the event. We are responsible for running resale with reasonable care and skill and for issuing valid replacement tickets. Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights.
