## 1. Headline: Gigvora's seller terms are a generation ahead

Gigvora's Seller Terms v2.4 (~10,500 words) were rewritten after an adversarial review that found 13 critical issues (the same ones this review finds in Gala Dock: "we decide/hold", set-off of protected money, no contract between the two users, consumer arbitration). Gala Dock's Provider Terms (~1,700 words, 2026-10) still describe the pre-DEC-107 model and aren't published.

## 3. Where Gala Dock needs more than Gigvora (physical events)
Gigvora is mostly remote knowledge work. Gala Dock suppliers and venues create physical-safety risk, so v3 adds things Gigvora doesn't need:
- licensing (premises licence, DPS, TENs, alcohol age verification), fire safety and capacity, Martyn's Law for qualifying premises/events (verify commencement);
- food hygiene and allergen information (incl. PPDS labelling);
- employers' liability insurance (compulsory where employees), product liability for food and merch;
- event-day obligations (arrival, setup, check-in tools), standby and emergency commitments;
- damage deposits and house rules (venues);
- safeguarding at children's events (DBS is the supplier's duty, not Gala Dock's);
- ticket chargeback holds and scanner results (organisers);
- PORR immovable-property reporting (venues).

## 4. Things Gala Dock does better or the same
- Objective reserves, no discretionary fraud reserve — same model.
- Seller fee cap 8%, fee snapshot, pro-rata fee refund — same.
- Two-person award check, consumer arbitration tiers — same.
- Force-majeure fee waiver for organisers (objective definition, evidence, 5-day decision, re-review) — Gigvora has no equivalent; keep.
- Venue BEO versioning, allergen accuracy — event-specific strengths.
