# Gala Dock Venue Terms (v3 draft, gd-ven-3.0)

> **DRAFT v3: not in force, not published.** Q54 legal review, 2026-11-14 (AI review, not legal advice). Replaces Venue Marketplace Terms 2026-10. Adds to the Supplier Terms for venues and space hire; on a venue matter these terms prevail. **Changes:** capacity and fire-safety responsibility for Gala Dock tools (§1.4); Martyn's Law (§2.4, verify); deposits (§5); DEC-107 payouts (§6); PORR property reporting (§7).

## 1. Listing a venue
1.1 You must have the right to hire out the venue and spaces you list, as owner, tenant or operator with authority, with any consent your lease or landlord requires.
1.2 Your listing must accurately state: capacities for each layout, which must not exceed your licensed or fire-risk-assessed capacity; access, setup and breakdown windows and curfews; noise limits; accessibility (step-free access, accessible toilets, hearing loops); power, rigging and load-in; facilities; and what is included in the price.
1.3 Floor plans, venue maps and structural layouts you publish must reflect the real space. Organisers are responsible for event overlays, but you must tell them about any constraint an overlay breaches (for example a blocked fire exit).
1.4 **Gala Dock's tools don't check compliance.** Seat maps, plan builders and capacity counters use the figures you and organisers enter. Gala Dock doesn't verify capacities, fire safety, structural loads or licensing, and its tools are not a fire risk assessment.

## 2. Licensing and safety
2.1 Gala Dock holds no premises licence and doesn't authorise licensable activities. You are responsible for your premises licence or club premises certificate and its conditions, the designated premises supervisor, temporary event notices, music and performance licensing, fire safety (Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005), health and safety and capacity.
2.2 Bar packages must comply with your licence conditions, the mandatory age-verification condition (Challenge 21/25), permitted hours and any jurisdiction rules shown in Gala Dock. We may block a package that breaches those rules.
2.3 Share relevant safety information with organisers: fire risk assessment constraints, evacuation routes, stewarding ratios, and conditions on special effects, pyrotechnics or naked flames.
2.4 Where the Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025 applies to your premises or an event [verify commencement], you are responsible for its duties and must tell organisers what they need to do.

## 3. Rate cards, holds and conflicts
3.1 Prices come from your rate cards (day parts, minimum spend, deposits, seasonal and day-of-week rules, add-ons). An accepted quote is binding.
3.2 Holds reserve a space for the stated period. Gala Dock detects calendar conflicts across linked and combinable spaces and won't let you double-confirm. You must block bookings you take outside Gala Dock in your calendar.
3.3 If you can't honour a confirmed booking, tell the customer and Gala Dock immediately. You may offer alternatives; the customer is entitled to a full refund if they don't accept one.

## 4. Event orders, catering and menus
Banquet event orders and catering selections are versioned. Once both parties approve, an event order forms part of the booking. Changes need a new version and, where prices change, the customer's confirmation. Menus must carry accurate information on the 14 major allergens; dietary labels must be truthful.

## 5. Damage, deposits and house rules
5.1 House rules and any security or damage deposit must be shown in the quote before the customer accepts.
5.2 A damage deposit taken through Gala Dock is a payment into your Stripe account like any other and follows the Payment Terms. A deposit you take outside Gala Dock is outside the Payment Terms.
5.3 Raise damage claims within 7 days after the event, with evidence, through Gala Dock. You may keep from a deposit only amounts the customer accepts, the parties settle, or an independent body decides; disputed claims follow the Disputes Policy.

## 6. Payouts
Venue hire payments are paid out under the Payment Terms. Money charged more than 58 days before the event may be paid out at the hold limit, before the event (Payment Terms 8.2); you remain liable to refund the customer if the booking doesn't go ahead.

## 7. Tax reporting for venue hire
Venue and space hire is "rental of immovable property" under the Platform Operators (Due Diligence and Reporting Requirements) Regulations 2023. In addition to the information in Supplier Terms 5.7, you must give us the address of each space you list and, if available, its land registration number. A venue operator for which Gala Dock facilitates more than 2,000 rentals of one listing in a year may be excluded [verify]; we'll tell you if so.
