## 1. Purpose and scope
Gala Dock is used to plan events that children and adults at risk may attend (children's parties, school events, community festivals) and to message between customers, organisers and suppliers. This policy sets out how Blackwellen Limited prevents, recognises and responds to abuse, exploitation and harm connected with the platform. It applies to our staff, contractors and administrators, and sets expectations for every user.
Gala Dock doesn't run events, employ event staff for organisers, or carry out regulated activity with children or adults at risk. Organisers, venues and suppliers are responsible for safeguarding at their events, including DBS checks where the law requires them. Gala Dock doesn't collect or check DBS certificates.

## 2. Principles
The welfare of children and adults at risk is paramount. Everyone has a right to protection from abuse whatever their age, disability, sex, gender reassignment, race, religion or belief, or sexual orientation. We act promptly, share information only where necessary and lawful, and keep proper records.

## 3. What we expect of users
- Organisers and suppliers working with children or adults at risk must follow their own safeguarding policies and legal duties, including safer recruitment and DBS checks where applicable.
- Don't use Gala Dock messaging to contact children directly. Accounts are for adults (18+).
- Report concerns immediately with **Report** or support@galadock.com. **If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.**

## 4. How we respond
1. **Receive.** Reports go to the trust and safety queue; safeguarding reports are prioritised and recorded (what, when, by whom).
2. **Assess.** The lead (or deputy) decides whether there is a risk of significant harm. Immediate risk: we contact the police (999) or the local authority's children's or adult services. We don't investigate crimes ourselves.
3. **Protect.** We may suspend accounts, hide listings or events, restrict messaging and preserve evidence.
4. **Messages.** Staff can't browse conversations. A trained administrator may request case-linked break-glass access with written justification, second-administrator approval for safeguarding cases and step-up verification; time-limited, read-only, every conversation logged.
5. **Refer and share** with the police, local authority designated officers (LADO) and other agencies where the law requires or permits (UK GDPR Art 6(1)(c), (d), (e); DPA 2018 Sch 1 para 18).
6. **Record and review.** Every case is recorded with decisions and reasons, kept securely under the Data Retention Schedule; the lead reviews case handling quarterly.
7. **Child sexual abuse material.** If we detect CSEA content, we preserve it securely, remove access, report to the National Crime Agency as the Online Safety Act 2023 requires [verify the s.66 reporting regulations' commencement] and to the police, and never forward or copy it except as required.

## 5. Notifying affected users
Where lawful and safe, we tell users after an investigation closes if their messages were accessed; we don't where it could put someone at risk or prejudice a criminal investigation, and we record why.

## 6. Staff
Staff and administrators with trust and safety access complete safeguarding and data protection training at induction and yearly. Admin access needs MFA; break-glass access is reviewed by the DPO.

## 7. Review
Reviewed yearly, or sooner after a serious case or change in law.
