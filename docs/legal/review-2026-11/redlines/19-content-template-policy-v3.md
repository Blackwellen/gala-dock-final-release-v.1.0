# Gala Dock Content and Template Policy (v3 draft, gd-ctp-3.0)

> **DRAFT v3: not in force, not published.** Q54 legal review, 2026-11-14 (AI review, not legal advice). Replaces Content & Template Policy 2026-10 with light changes: licence chain and moral-rights waiver for published templates (§3.3), AI output (§1.4).

## 1. Your content, your rights
1.1 You keep ownership of what you upload or create in Gala Dock: images, designs, text, menus, floor plans and templates.
1.2 You must have the rights to everything you use: your own work, licensed stock, or content you have permission to use, including photos of people (with consent where needed), logos and trade marks, artwork, fonts and music.
1.3 Gala Dock's fonts and icons are provided under open licences; you may use them in designs made in Gala Dock, including for commercial event marketing. Uploaded custom fonts must be licensed for embedding and web use.
1.4 Gala Dock doesn't generate photographs of real people or places. If you use AI tools elsewhere, you're responsible for the output and its rights, and for labelling it where that avoids misleading people.

## 2. Private by default
Designs and uploads are private to you and your workspace unless you share or publish them. Private files are served only through short-lived, permission-checked links.

## 3. Publishing templates
3.1 You can make a template private, workspace-only, link-only or public.
3.2 When you publish for link or public use, Gala Dock replaces event-specific details (name, date, venue, prices, links and QR destinations) with placeholders and removes images from your private uploads unless you choose to publish a public copy. Check the template has no personal data, confidential information or content you can't licence.
3.3 **Licence to other users.** By publishing for link or public use, you grant Gala Dock the right to make the template available and grant every Gala Dock user a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to copy, adapt and use it in their own designs for their own events while it is published, and indefinitely for copies already made. To the extent the law allows, you waive moral rights against those users for these uses. Users get their own copy; your original isn't changed.
3.4 You can unpublish at any time; copies already made remain with their users.

## 4. What isn't allowed
Content that: infringes copyright, trade marks, design or personality rights; is unlawful, hateful, sexually explicit, violent or harassing; is misleading (fake endorsements, false sold-out claims, invented scarcity); contains malicious code, or QR codes or links to phishing or malware; or imitates Gala Dock, another brand or a real person to deceive. QR codes must point to destinations you control or are authorised to use.

## 5. Reports and moderation
Anyone can report a public template. Three independent open reports hide it pending review. Moderators can hide, remove or reinstate templates and restrict creators who repeatedly breach this policy, with a recorded reason and a right of appeal. Copyright complaints follow the IP and Takedown Procedure.
