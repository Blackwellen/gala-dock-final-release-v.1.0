## 1. What Gala Dock is

1.1 Gala Dock is a platform for planning events, selling and buying tickets, and finding, booking and paying venues and suppliers.

1.2 **Who does what.** **Organisers** run events and sell tickets. **Suppliers** (caterers, entertainers, AV, staffing, equipment, décor and other services) and **venues** sell services and space hire. Together they are **sellers**. **Customers** buy. Organisers can also buy from suppliers and venues.

1.3 **Sellers are independent businesses.** Sellers use our software under their own agreements with us. They are not our employees, workers, agents, partners or contractors, and they can't bind us. When you buy a ticket or book a venue or supplier, your contract for the event, venue or service is **with the seller**, and the seller is responsible for delivering it. The seller sells in its own name. We act for the seller only to let you conclude that contract on our checkout in the seller's name, and we never sell tickets or services ourselves except our own plans and credit packs.

1.4 **Your money goes to the seller.** When you pay a seller, your payment goes through Stripe directly into **the seller's own Stripe account**. The seller is the merchant of record. Gala Dock never holds your money, and never decides where it goes. Our only money from your payment is our fee, which Stripe deducts from the same payment. The Payment Terms explain when the seller is paid out.

1.5 **Not escrow, not insurance.** Gala Dock is not an escrow service, a bank, an insurer or a payment service provider. Stripe Payments UK Ltd, an authorised payment institution [owner/counsel: confirm Stripe's exact authorisation], provides the payment services.
