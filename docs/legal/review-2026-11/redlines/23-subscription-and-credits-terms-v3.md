# Gala Dock Subscription and Credits Terms (v3 draft, NEW, gd-sub-1.0)

> **DRAFT v3: not in force, not published.** Q54 legal review, 2026-11-14 (AI review, not legal advice). **New document**: the Payment Terms v2 refer to "subscription terms" that don't exist (behaviour-mismatches M-80). Written to match Q60/Q67: plans and AI credit packs sold by Gala Dock as merchant; Stripe customer portal for cards, invoices and cancellation; plan limits enforced on new creation/publish only; automatic tax behind `BILLING_AUTOMATIC_TAX`; downgrade undo, interval switch prorated, duplicate subscriptions cancelled. **DMCCA subscription contract rules (Part 4 Ch 2): confirm commencement and whether any consumers buy plans; if so add reminders, a cooling-off period and easy exit before launch (CQ-11c).**

## 1. Who these terms apply to
They apply when you buy a Gala Dock plan or AI credit pack. Gala Dock (Blackwellen Limited) is the seller and merchant of record. Plans and credits are sold for business use by organisers, suppliers, venues and partners. [If consumers can buy, the consumer additions in §9 apply.]

## 2. Plans
2.1 Plans (Starter, Pro, Business, Enterprise) give software features and limits: active events (drafts and archived events don't count), team seats (owner, active members and pending invites), published listings, storage, AI credits, API access and advanced reporting, as shown on the pricing page at purchase.
2.2 **Limits** apply when you create or publish something new. Reaching a limit never deletes or unpublishes what you already have; you'll be offered an upgrade.
2.3 Plans don't buy bookings, work or organic search ranking. Plan level sets the commission rate on bookings (Supplier Terms 5.3).

## 3. Price, billing and VAT
3.1 Prices for business users are shown excluding VAT. VAT is added at the rate that applies to you (UK VAT for UK customers; reverse charge for business customers elsewhere who give a valid tax number; local VAT or GST where the law requires) and shown on your invoice.
3.2 Plans are billed in advance, monthly or yearly, to your saved payment method, and **renew automatically** for the same period until cancelled. We remind you [7] days before a yearly renewal.
3.3 Changing interval or upgrading takes effect at once, with a prorated charge or credit. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current period; you can undo a scheduled downgrade before then.
3.4 If a payment fails, we retry and tell you; you can pay from the link we send. If payment isn't made within [14] days, the plan moves to the free tier (limits apply as in 2.2).

## 4. Cancelling
4.1 Cancel any time in Settings → Payments & Billing (Stripe customer portal). Your plan stays active to the end of the paid period.
4.2 We don't refund part-periods unless the law requires it or we failed to provide the service.

## 5. AI credits
5.1 Plans include a monthly allowance of AI credits for Gala Agent (organisers and suppliers only); you can buy credit packs.
5.2 Credits are used oldest first. Unused **purchased** credits expire **12 months** after purchase; included monthly credits expire at the end of the month [confirm].
5.3 Credits wrongly used because of our fault are restored. A refunded credit pack removes its unused credits.
5.4 The credit cost of each quality mode is shown in the app; we won't change it without notice.

## 6. Partners
If you were referred by a partner, the partner may earn commission on what you pay. Partners see totals only, never your workspace name.

## 7. Changes
We give at least 30 days' notice of price changes, before your next renewal. You can cancel before the change applies.

## 8. Liability and law
Terms of Service §§12–15 apply.

## 9. Consumers [only if consumers can buy]
9.1 Prices include VAT.
9.2 You may cancel a new subscription within 14 days of purchase and receive a refund, less a proportionate amount for any service used if you asked for it to start immediately. You'll confirm with a tick that you want it to start immediately.
9.3 For AI credit packs (digital content), you'll be asked to agree to immediate supply and acknowledge that you lose the 14-day right once supply starts.
