# Gala Dock Standard Booking Terms (v3 draft, NEW, gd-sbt-1.0)

> **DRAFT v3: not in force, not published.** Q54 legal review, 2026-11-14 (AI review, not legal advice). **New document** closing the gap that Gigvora's barrister review called critical #8 and this review calls X-3: the dispute rules, default rule and allocation of risk between buyer and seller need a contract **between the two of them**. The seller agrees to these terms (as a standing offer) when it publishes its first listing or event or sets up payouts; the buyer agrees at checkout or quote acceptance; each booking or ticket contract then includes them. Gala Dock isn't a party but may rely on clauses 9–10 (Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999). Counsel: CQ-4.

## 1. What these terms are
1.1 These terms form part of every contract between a **buyer** and a **seller** made through Gala Dock (ticket purchases, venue and supplier bookings, change orders and organiser invoices paid by card), unless the seller's saved terms, shown before the buyer committed, say something different that is allowed by clause 1.3.
1.2 Priority: (a) the buyer's statutory rights; (b) these terms; (c) the seller's saved booking terms (quote, cancellation policy, event conditions, house rules); (d) listing descriptions.
1.3 A seller's own terms may add to these terms or be more generous to the buyer, but may not exclude clauses 4, 5, 7, 8 or 9 below.

## 2. The contract
2.1 The contract is between the buyer and the seller only, made in the seller's name. Gala Dock isn't the seller and isn't liable for the seller's performance.
2.2 The contract includes everything saved with the booking at acceptance and the event conditions shown before a ticket purchase.

## 3. Payment
3.1 The buyer pays through Gala Dock into the seller's Stripe account, on the schedule in the quote or at checkout. Payment through Gala Dock discharges the buyer's obligation to pay the seller for that amount.
3.2 The buyer's booking or admin fee is Gala Dock's charge, collected by the seller for Gala Dock (Payment Terms 2A).

## 4. Seller doesn't deliver
4.1 If the seller cancels, doesn't turn up, or delivers something materially different from what was booked, the buyer is entitled to a full refund of the amounts paid for the part not properly delivered (or a fair price reduction for part delivery), without affecting any other legal remedy.
4.2 Claims for other losses (for example the cost of a replacement supplier) are between buyer and seller. Any limit on the seller's liability in its saved terms applies only if it is fair and, for consumers, never limits liability the law doesn't allow to be limited.

## 5. Buyer cancels
5.1 The seller's saved cancellation policy applies if it was shown before the buyer committed and is fair.
5.2 **If there is no saved policy**, the buyer gets back what it paid, less the seller's reasonable costs actually incurred and evidenced, and any loss the seller can't avoid by re-booking the date.

## 6. Events that can't go ahead (force majeure)
If the event can't lawfully or safely go ahead for reasons outside both parties' reasonable control (for example a government or licensing order, the venue being closed by fire or flood, a red weather warning), either party may end the booking. The buyer gets back amounts paid for services not delivered, less reasonable costs the seller has actually and unavoidably incurred before the event and can evidence. Neither party is otherwise liable to the other for the cancellation.

## 7. Disputes
7.1 Buyer and seller agree that disputes about the contract follow the Gala Dock Disputes Policy as it applies to them, including: payout waits while a dispute is open; Gala Dock may recommend but doesn't decide; money moves only on acceptance, settlement, an independent award or the default rule; consumers are never required to arbitrate claims of £5,000 or less; and, between business parties who have accepted them, the deadline rules and the arbitration clause.
7.2 **Default rule.** If a dispute is still open at the payment provider's hold limit, the disputed amount is refunded to the buyer and the rest paid to the seller. This isn't a decision. The buyer remains liable to the seller for the refunded amount unless a court, arbitrator or ADR body decides otherwise or the parties agree; Gala Dock may give the seller the buyer's contact details for that purpose.

## 8. Chargebacks on tickets
If a buyer disputes a ticket payment with its card issuer, the tickets are put on hold until the dispute is decided, restored if the seller wins, and cancelled if the card issuer reverses the payment (Ticket Terms §8).

## 9. Records
Buyer and seller agree that the Gala Dock booking record (saved terms, messages, timeline, file fingerprints and case pack) may be used as evidence of what was agreed and said.

## 10. Gala Dock
Gala Dock isn't a party to the contract and has no liability under it. Clauses 7, 9 and this clause 10 may be relied on by Gala Dock.

## 11. Law
The law of England and Wales governs these terms, except that a consumer keeps the protection of the mandatory law of the place where they live. Courts: as the Disputes Policy says.
