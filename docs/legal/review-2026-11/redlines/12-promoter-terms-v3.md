# Gala Dock Promoter Terms (v3 draft, gd-pro-3.0)

> **DRAFT v3: not in force, not published.** Q54 legal review, 2026-11-14 (AI review, not legal advice). Replaces Promoter Terms 2026-10. **Changes:** consent-based cookie (built); organiser approves sales and runs payouts (Q66/Q68); partial-refund reversal (202611146204); Gala Dock is not liable for the organiser's commission; liability and law added.

These terms apply to people and businesses who promote events on Gala Dock using **promoter links**, and to organisers who invite them. They form part of the Terms of Service.

## 1. How promotion works
1.1 An organiser invites you to promote an event and sets the commission (a percentage or fixed amount per paid ticket), shown in your portal.
1.2 You share your promoter link (`galadock.com/r/<code>`). If a visitor agrees to the referral cookie (`gd_ref`, 30 days), a primary ticket purchase for that event within 30 days is credited to you. Without consent, no credit is recorded.
1.3 Credit is given only for completed, paid primary-ticket orders for that event. Refunded (in whole or part), cancelled or charged-back orders are reversed in proportion. Resale purchases and free tickets don't earn commission.

## 2. Payment
2.1 **The organiser owes your commission**, not Gala Dock. The organiser approves attributed sales and runs payouts in Gala Dock, after the event has taken place and refund risk has passed. Gala Dock records attribution and the organiser's payout runs; it isn't liable if the organiser doesn't pay.
2.1A **Security reviews.** If a security review of your Gala Dock account is open (for example after signs that someone else has accessed it), Gala Dock may stop a promoter payout being marked payable or paid until a person completes the review, and tells you and the organiser why. The organiser still owes approved commission. *[Q74 2026-11-14: owner decision D5]*
2.2 You are responsible for your own tax and, if VAT-registered, for invoicing the organiser.

## 3. Rules
- Promote honestly: don't misstate prices, line-ups, availability or scarcity, or claim to be the organiser.
- **Say you're paid**, clearly (for example "#ad"), following the CAP Code and ASA guidance.
- No spam, unsolicited messages without consent (PECR), fake accounts, cookie stuffing, self-referrals or buying through your own link.
- Follow advertising rules for paid ads; don't bid on "Gala Dock" brand terms.
- Only promote events you've been invited to.

## 4. Privacy
We record the referral with a consented first-party cookie and a privacy-preserving visitor hash. Promoters get only aggregated counts and amounts, never buyers' personal data.

## 5. Ending
Organisers can end a promotion at any time. Approved commission on qualifying orders remains payable by the organiser. We may suspend promoters who break these terms and reverse commission obtained through breaches.

## 6. Liability and law
Gala Dock's liability to promoters is limited as in Terms of Service §12 (business users). You indemnify Gala Dock against claims arising from your promotional activity. England and Wales law; courts of England and Wales.
