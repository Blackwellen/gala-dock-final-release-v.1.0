These terms apply to people and organisations who join the Gala Dock Partner Programme to refer new workspaces, resell plans, manage workspaces for clients or build integrations ("**you**"). They form part of the Terms of Service. Event promoters are covered by the Promoter Terms. The contracting party is Blackwellen Limited (company 16482166, VAT GB508480488), 61 Bridge Street, Kington, Herefordshire, HR5 3DJ.

## 1. Partner types
- **Referral partners** earn commission on referred workspaces that become paying customers.
- **Solutions partners** set up and manage workspaces for clients with the client's authority.
- **Technology partners** build integrations with the Gala Dock API, MCP server and webhooks.

## 2. Joining
2.1 You apply online. We approve or decline applications, giving reasons if we decline. We may ask for identity, business and tax details.
2.2 You must be 18 or over and act in the course of a business (including as a sole trader). You confirm on application whether you are VAT-registered.
2.3 You must not be a sanctioned person or resident in a sanctioned territory (Terms §2.2).
2.4 You are an independent business, not our employee, worker, agent or partner in law, and you can't bind us or make commitments on our behalf.

## 3. Your link and attribution
3.1 Referrals are tracked by your link or code. A visitor is credited to you only if they agree to the partner cookie (Cookie Policy, `gd_partner`, 30 days) and create a **new** workspace within that period. Accounts that existed before the click aren't credited.
3.2 A referral qualifies when the referred workspace starts a paid plan [owner: within 60 days of sign-up — align with the build].
3.3 No commission is earned on: your own or connected workspaces (self-referral); fake, fraudulent or duplicate accounts; existing customers; or accounts obtained by spam, cookie stuffing, bidding on "Gala Dock" brand terms, or misleading claims.

## 4. Commission
4.1 **Base.** Commission is a percentage of the amounts referred workspaces pay Gala Dock for **subscriptions and credit packs**, **net of VAT**, refunds and chargebacks. It doesn't include ticket, booking or seller fees.
4.2 **Grade.** The percentage depends on your grade, set automatically by how many of your referred workspaces are active and paying:

| Grade | Active referred workspaces | Commission |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 | 6% |
| 2 | 5–14 | 7% |
| 3 | 15–29 | 8% |
| 4 | 30–49 | 9% |
| 5 | 50 or more | 10% |

4.3 Your grade is recalculated when a referred workspace starts or stops paying, and the new rate applies from then on (your grade can go down). A signed order form can fix a different rate.
4.4 **VAT.** If you are VAT-registered in the UK, we add UK VAT to your commission and pay it with the commission under the self-billing agreement in Schedule 1. If you are VAT-registered elsewhere, no UK VAT is added; we account for any VAT under the reverse charge. If you aren't VAT-registered, no VAT is added.

## 5. Earning hold, statements and minimum
5.1 **Hold.** Commission becomes payable **60 days** after the payment it relates to, so that refunds and chargebacks can be taken into account.
5.2 **Statements.** Cleared commission goes onto a monthly statement in your portal, which shows totals and statuses only. We don't show the names of referred workspaces.
5.3 **Minimum payout.** A statement is paid only if its balance is at least the minimum payout shown in your portal: the amount at which our payout costs are no more than 5% of the payout, and never less than **£50**. A smaller balance rolls forward to the next statement.

## 6. Clawbacks and negative balances
6.1 If a payment that earned commission is refunded or charged back (in whole or part), the commission on that part is reversed, even if it was already paid.
6.2 Reversals are deducted from your next statements. If a statement is negative, the negative balance carries forward and is deducted from later commission.
6.3 You authorise us to reverse the most recent Stripe transfer to you to recover a reversed amount where Stripe allows it. If neither is possible within 90 days, you must repay on invoice within 14 days.
6.4 We may also recover commission paid because of your breach of 3.3 or our error.

## 7. Payment
7.1 We pay by **Stripe Connect transfer** to your own connected account (no Stripe dashboard), then a payout to your bank. You must complete Stripe's checks. Until your account is set up, commission accrues and is held.
7.2 If Stripe can't support your country, we will agree another way to pay you; until then commission accrues.
7.3 **Holding payment.** We may hold a statement while we investigate a reasonable suspicion of breach of 3.3 or fraud, for up to 90 days, telling you the reason; or where a law, regulator or Stripe requires it.
7.4 You are responsible for your own income tax, National Insurance and other taxes.

## 8. Marketing rules
8.1 Describe yourself as a "Gala Dock Partner" only, using our logos under the brand guidelines. Don't suggest you are Gala Dock.
8.2 **Disclose that you are paid** to recommend Gala Dock, clearly and up front (for example "#ad" or "affiliate link"), following the CAP Code and ASA guidance.
8.3 Make only claims you can prove. Don't: claim earnings, savings or results you can't evidence; invent reviews or endorsements; describe Gala Dock as escrow, a bank, an insurer or a regulated payment service; quote prices to consumers without VAT.
8.4 Don't send electronic marketing without the recipient's consent or another lawful basis under PECR. You are the controller of your own marketing data.

## 9. Acting for clients and API use
9.1 Solutions partners access a client's workspace only as a member invited by the client, with the role the client gives, following the client's instructions and the Data Processing Addendum obligations that flow down.
9.2 API and MCP keys act with their creator's permissions and scopes; commitments need approval in the app; rate limits apply. Store keys and webhook secrets securely, verify webhook signatures and don't resell API access.

## 10. Data and confidentiality
Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential. You receive totals only unless a client authorises more. You may keep referral totals and statements for your records.

## 11. Audit and records
We may ask for reasonable information to check compliance with 3.3 and 8. You must keep records of your promotional activity for 12 months.

## 12. Term and termination
12.1 Either party may end these terms on 30 days' notice, or immediately for material breach, fraud or a serious breach of 8.
12.2 On termination, cleared commission that isn't reversed under 6 is paid at the next statement run. Commission obtained through breach is forfeited.

## 13. Liability and indemnity
13.1 Each party's total liability under these terms in any 12 months is limited to the greater of the commission paid or payable in the previous 12 months and £1,000, except liability that can't be limited by law.
13.2 You indemnify us against claims, fines (to the extent lawful) and costs arising from your marketing, your breach of 8 or your breach of law.

## 14. Law
England and Wales; the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
