# Gala Dock Intellectual Property and Takedown Procedure (v3 draft, gd-ip-3.0)

> **DRAFT v3: not in force, not published.** Q54 legal review, 2026-11-14 (AI review, not legal advice). Replaces IP & Takedown 2026-10 with light changes: report form, statement of reasons, DSA-style notice fields for EU notices (if EU users accepted).

Gala Dock hosts content uploaded by users (event pages, listings, images, designs, templates and reviews). We act quickly on valid notices, in line with the Electronic Commerce (EC Directive) Regulations 2002.

## 1. Reporting infringement
Use **Report → Intellectual property** on the content, galadock.com/report, or email legal@galadock.com. Include: (1) your name and contact details, and the rights holder's if you act for them; (2) the right you say is infringed (copyright work, trade mark, design); (3) the exact location (URL or template link); (4) why you believe it infringes; (5) a statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act.

## 2. What we do
- We review notices promptly, normally within 2 working days.
- If the notice is complete and the content appears to infringe, we remove or disable it and tell the uploader, with the notice details and our reasons (we may withhold your personal contact details).
- Public templates may be hidden immediately while we review.
- We tell you what we decided.

## 3. Counter-notice
If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, reply with your explanation and evidence (for example a licence). We may restore it if the counter-notice is credible, after telling the complainant, unless they show they have started legal proceedings.

## 4. Repeat infringers
Accounts with repeated valid notices may have publishing restricted, or be suspended or terminated.

## 5. Misuse
Knowingly false notices may be unlawful; we may decline notices from repeat abusers of this process.
