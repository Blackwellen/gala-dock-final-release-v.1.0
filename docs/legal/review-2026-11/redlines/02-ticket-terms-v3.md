# Gala Dock Ticket Terms (v3 draft, gd-tt-3.0)

> **DRAFT v3: not in force, not published.** Q54 legal review, 2026-11-14 (AI review, not legal advice). Replaces `/ticket-terms` (2026-10). New: seller identity (intro), chargeback holds (§8, owner decision 2026-11-14), resale as built (§7), corrected CCRs citation (§2, verify).

These terms apply when you buy, hold, transfer or resell tickets on Gala Dock. They sit with the Terms of Service, the Payment Terms and the Standard Booking Terms.

**Who sells you the ticket.** The **event organiser** sells you the ticket in its own name and is responsible, and liable, for the event. The organiser's name is shown on the event page, at checkout and on your receipt. Gala Dock runs the platform and lets you conclude the contract with the organiser on our checkout; we don't sell tickets ourselves. Your payment goes directly to the organiser's own Stripe account. The £1.00 booking fee is Gala Dock's charge for our booking service.

## 1. Buying tickets

1.1 The ticket price, the £1.00 booking fee per paid ticket (including VAT), and the total are shown from the first price you see, and again before you pay. Free tickets carry no fee.

1.2 Your chosen tickets or seats are held for a short time while you check out; the countdown is on screen. If the hold expires, they are released, and any payment that completes after that is refunded automatically.

1.3 There may be a limit on tickets per order. Using bots or other software to get around purchase limits is a criminal offence.

1.4 Your order is confirmed only when payment succeeds. We email you a confirmation with the organiser's details and these terms.

1.5 **Event conditions.** The organiser may set conditions such as age limits, ID checks, dress code, bag policy, or a ban on resale outside Gala Dock. Conditions shown on the event page before you pay form part of your ticket contract. Venues that sell alcohol must refuse service to anyone under 18 and anyone who can't show acceptable ID when asked.

## 2. No 14-day cancellation period

2.1 Tickets are for leisure services on a specific date, so you don't have the usual 14-day right to cancel (Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, regulation 28(1)(b) [counsel: verify paragraph]). You can still transfer or resell your ticket (§§4, 7).

2.2 For on-demand online content that isn't for a specific date, the organiser's page will say whether a cancellation right applies.

## 3. If the event is cancelled, postponed or changed

3.1 **Cancelled:** the organiser refunds the ticket price **and** the booking fee to your original payment method, within 14 days of the cancellation. The refund comes from the organiser's Stripe account, where ticket money stays until after the event.

3.2 **Postponed or moved:** your ticket is valid for the new date or place. If you can't attend, you can ask for a refund within the period announced, which will be at least 14 days after we tell you.

3.3 **Materially changed** (for example a different date, a significantly different venue, or the main advertised act no longer appearing): you can ask for a refund, as for a postponement, in addition to any statutory rights.

3.4 **If the organiser can't pay.** If an organiser doesn't refund you, you keep your rights against the organiser and your right to ask your card issuer for a chargeback (and, for purchases of £100–£30,000 per item on a credit card, a claim under section 75 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974). We'll give you the records of your order. Gala Dock doesn't owe you refunds the organiser owes.

## 4. Your ticket

4.1 Your ticket has a secure QR code that refreshes regularly. Screenshots may not scan, so show it from your account or wallet.

4.2 You can transfer a ticket to someone else until shortly before the event. The new holder gets a new code and the old code stops working. A transferred ticket remains linked to the original payment: if that payment is disputed, the transferred ticket is affected too (§8).

4.3 A ticket admits one person once. Copied or fraudulent tickets will be refused.

## 5. At the event
5.1 The organiser and venue may refuse entry or remove you for safety, licensing or conduct reasons set out in the event conditions or required by law, without a refund where that is fair.

## 6. Seated events
6.1 A seat or table you choose is held during checkout and assigned when payment succeeds. The organiser may move you to an equal or better seat for safety or operational reasons.

## 7. Resale through Gala Dock

7.1 Where the organiser allows it, you can list a ticket on Gala Dock's resale. You sell at **the price you paid** for the ticket. If that is above the organiser's resale cap (a percentage of face value), you can't list it.

7.2 **How a resale works.** When a buyer buys your listing, the organiser sells them a new ticket for the same place, your ticket is cancelled, and the organiser **refunds you the price you paid to your original payment method**. Your original £1.00 booking fee isn't refunded. The buyer pays the price plus a £1.00 booking fee.

7.3 **What buyers see before paying** (Consumer Rights Act 2015, section 90): the seat, row or standing area; any restrictions on use; the original face value; a ticket reference [counsel CQ-12: masked reference vs unique ticket number]; and that the ticket is sold by the event organiser through Gala Dock resale.

7.4 You must not resell elsewhere in breach of the organiser's conditions. If the organiser's conditions shown before you bought allow it, a ticket offered for resale in breach may be cancelled.

7.5 Resale isn't available for designated football matches or where the law prohibits it.

## 8. Card disputes (chargebacks)

8.1 If you, or someone using your card, disputes the payment for tickets with the card issuer, **the tickets in that order are put on hold** while the dispute is open. That includes tickets you transferred. A ticket on hold won't admit anyone ("Ticket on hold — see box office"), and can't be transferred or resold. Any resale listing is withdrawn.

8.2 **If the dispute is decided in the organiser's favour**, the tickets become valid again with the same codes.

8.3 **If the card issuer reverses the payment**, the tickets are cancelled and may be sold again.

8.4 A ticket that someone else bought through resale is never affected by a dispute on your original payment.

8.5 If you dispute a payment by mistake, ask your card issuer to withdraw it; when the issuer closes it, the hold is lifted. Contact us first if something has gone wrong; we can often help faster. Nothing here limits your right to dispute a payment.

## 9. Help
For the event itself, contact the organiser through the event page. For orders, refunds, transfers and holds, contact support@galadock.com.

## 10. Responsibility
10.1 The organiser is responsible for the event. Gala Dock is responsible for running the ticketing platform with reasonable care and skill and for issuing valid tickets and replacement tickets. Our liability is set out in the Terms of Service §12. Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights.
