# Gala Dock Cancellation and Refund Policy (v3 draft, gd-crp-3.0)

> **DRAFT v3: not in force, not published.** Q54 legal review, 2026-11-14 (AI review, not legal advice). Replaces Cancellation & Refund Policy 2026-10. **Changes:** Gala Dock no longer "reviews" (decides) refunds when no policy exists or on force majeure — defaults now live in the Standard Booking Terms; 24-hour window; refunds within 14 days; fees for plans/credits moved to the Subscription and Credits Terms.

This policy explains what happens to your money when a ticket or booking is cancelled, changed or goes wrong. It doesn't remove any legal rights.

## 1. Who sets the rules
- **Tickets:** the Ticket Terms and the organiser's event conditions shown before you buy.
- **Venue and supplier bookings:** the seller's cancellation policy saved with your booking, and the Standard Booking Terms.
- **Gala Dock plans and credits:** the Subscription and Credits Terms.

## 2. Tickets
| What happened | What you get |
|---|---|
| Organiser cancels | Full refund of the ticket price and booking fee to your original payment method |
| Date, time or venue changes, or a main act no longer appears | Keep your ticket, or ask for a refund within the period announced (at least 14 days after we tell you) |
| You can't attend | No automatic refund. Transfer the ticket, or resell it through Gala Dock where allowed (you get back what you paid; the original booking fee isn't refunded) |
| Event significantly different from what was advertised | Contact the organiser; if not resolved, open a dispute (24 hours after the event) or complain to us. Your statutory rights apply |

Refunds are sent within **14 days** of the cancellation or agreement; your bank may take a few more days to show them.

## 3. Venue and supplier bookings
3.1 **If you cancel:** the refund is worked out from the seller's policy saved with your booking, by the number of days between your cancellation and the event start. The screen shows the exact amount before you confirm. Amounts marked non-refundable are kept only if the quote said so clearly before you accepted.
3.2 **If there is no saved policy:** the Standard Booking Terms apply: you get back what you paid, less the seller's reasonable costs actually incurred and evidenced, and any loss the seller can't avoid by re-booking. If you don't agree the amount, either of you can open a dispute.
3.3 **If the seller cancels:** you get a full refund of everything paid for that booking, including our admin fee. Where available, we show replacements, including standby suppliers already lined up.
3.4 **Rescheduling** needs the seller's agreement. If the seller can't do the new date, 3.1 or 3.3 applies depending on who can't proceed.
3.5 **Events that can't go ahead (force majeure):** the Standard Booking Terms apply: you get back amounts paid for services not delivered, less reasonable costs the seller has actually and unavoidably incurred and can evidence. Gala Dock may recommend an amount; it doesn't decide.
3.6 **How refunds are paid:** from the seller's Stripe account to your original payment method. If the seller has already been paid out, the refund comes from its balance, reserve and later payouts (Payment Terms 6.4).

## 4. Problems with a delivered service
If a seller doesn't turn up, delivers something materially different, or doesn't use reasonable care and skill, open a **dispute** from the booking **within 24 hours after the event ends**. While it is open, the seller isn't paid out. The time limit affects only payout timing, not your legal rights.

## 5. Chargebacks
Please contact us before asking your bank for a chargeback; we can often resolve things faster. You can always ask your card issuer. While a chargeback is open, the related payment isn't paid out to the seller; for tickets, the tickets are put on hold (Ticket Terms §8).

## 6. Gala Dock's fees
- **Ticket booking fee (£1.00):** refunded when the organiser or Gala Dock cancels, a hold expired, the payment was duplicated or our error, or the law requires it. Not refunded on a resale or a refund you choose.
- **Booking admin fee (2.5%, min £1):** refunded when the seller or Gala Dock cancels or the seller fails to provide the service; otherwise not refundable.
- **Seller commission:** returned to the seller on any refunded part.
- **Organiser cancellations:** buyers are always refunded in full. What the organiser owes Gala Dock is in the Organiser Terms.

## 7. Contact
Help in the app or support@galadock.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled a refund, see the Complaints Policy.
