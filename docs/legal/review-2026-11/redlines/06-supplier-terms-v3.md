# Gala Dock Supplier Terms (v3 draft, gd-sup-3.0)

> **DRAFT v3: not in force, not published.** Q54 legal review, 2026-11-14 (AI review, not legal advice). Replaces Provider Marketplace Terms 2026-10 (never published). Benchmarked against Gigvora Seller Terms v2.4 (`../gigvora-benchmark.md`). Accepted with a tick when a supplier publishes its first listing or sets up payouts. Venues also accept the Venue Terms.

These terms apply when you list services and take bookings through Gala Dock as a **supplier** (for example catering, entertainment, AV, staffing, equipment, décor, transport and other services). They form part of the Terms of Service and incorporate the Payment Terms, Disputes Policy, Standard Booking Terms, Cancellation and Refund Policy, Acceptable Use and Online Safety Policy, Community and Review Standards and AI Terms. Contracting party: Blackwellen Limited (company 16482166, VAT GB508480488), 61 Bridge Street, Kington, Herefordshire, HR5 3DJ.

## 1. Our relationship
1.1 **You are an independent business.** You use our software; you don't work for us. You aren't our employee, worker, agent, partner, joint venturer or subcontractor, and nothing in the Legal Documents creates employment or a contract of service.
1.2 **Your freedom.** We have no obligation to offer you work and you have no obligation to accept any request, sign in or stay available. You set your prices, methods, hours, staff and tools. You may work through other platforms and for your own clients. We don't guarantee any bookings or income. We don't train, appraise, supervise or direct you.
1.3 **We don't find work for you.** Gala Dock provides a marketplace and tools. Our fees are for those tools, not for finding you work.
1.4 **The controls we apply, and why.** We apply only the controls needed to make payments work, keep users safe and prevent fraud: payments and payouts go through your own Stripe account on the Payment Terms triggers so customers can rely on them; messages and bookings stay on Gala Dock so there is a record for disputes and to stop payment-diversion fraud; response times and reliability affect search ranking only (Terms Schedule 1). We never suspend you for declining work or being unavailable.
1.5 If you're employed elsewhere, on a visa or a student, you must make sure you're allowed to work in this way. We don't check this for you.
1.6 **Trader status.** You tell us truthfully whether you act as a business. If you don't, you may offer services only to business customers [owner: or allow consumer sales with a non-trader warning, as built].

## 2. Listings
2.1 Listings must be accurate and current: inclusions, prices including all compulsory charges, minimums, service area, capacity, lead times, food allergen and dietary information, certifications, insurance and availability. Photos must show your real work or be labelled illustrative.
2.2 Keep your calendar current. Accepting a booking you can't deliver is a serious breach.
2.3 **Verification.** We may check your identity (through Stripe), business details, insurance and qualifications before your listings go live and afterwards. Badges show only what was checked and when.

## 3. Requests, quotes, holds and standby
3.1 Requests show a response deadline; your response rate and time affect your reliability score.
3.2 **Quotes** are binding offers for their validity period. They must include all compulsory charges, the deposit and balance schedule and your cancellation policy. For consumers they must include the information the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 require (Gala Dock's quote form collects it). Your cancellation policy must be fair: charges on cancellation must reflect your likely loss, and deposits may be non-refundable only if proportionate and clearly stated. We may refuse unfair policies.
3.3 Holds reserve capacity until expiry; standby commitments must be delivered if activated within their terms; emergency acceptances are commitments to deliver at the stated time.

## 4. Delivery, staff and insurance
4.1 Deliver as booked, with reasonable care and skill, on time, and in compliance with law, including food hygiene and allergen law (including prepacked-for-direct-sale labelling), health and safety, licensing, employment and right-to-work law.
4.2 Use the booking's event-day tools where required and tell the organiser promptly about delays or problems.
4.3 **Insurance.** You must hold: public liability insurance of at least **£[1,000,000 / 2,000,000]** per occurrence [owner decision D10: the platform currently checks £1m]; employers' liability insurance where the law requires (normally if you have employees); product liability cover if you supply food or goods; and any other cover the booking or the law requires. You must provide evidence when asked and tell us if cover lapses.
4.4 **Your team.** If people other than you deliver the service, you are responsible for them as for yourself: their contracts and status, pay (including minimum wage and holiday pay), PAYE and National Insurance, right-to-work checks, health and safety, safeguarding checks (including DBS where the law requires), and compliance with these terms.
4.5 **Off-payroll rules.** If you work through your own company for a medium or large organiser, the off-payroll working rules may apply; you must ask for and follow the organiser's status determination. Gala Dock isn't the client or fee-payer.

## 5. Payment, commission and payouts
5.1 Customers pay directly into your own Stripe account under the Payment Terms. You are the merchant of record. You must finish Stripe set-up before customers can pay you.
5.2 **Payouts** happen on the Payment Terms triggers (customer confirmation, or event end + 24 hours with no dispute; settlement; award; hold limit), less commission, refunds, chargebacks and any reserve.
5.3 **Commission** is a charge for the marketplace, software and payment tools, at the rate recorded on the booking when paid: **8% (Starter), 7% (Pro), 6% (Business or Enterprise)**, plus VAT where it applies, unless agreed otherwise in writing. It is deducted by Stripe as Gala Dock's application fee. Commission on refunded amounts is returned. Gala Dock pays Stripe's processing and Connect fees from its fee; Stripe's chargeback dispute fee (currently £20) falls on your balance.
5.4 **Customer admin fee.** Customers pay Gala Dock a booking admin fee of 2.5% of each payment (minimum £1), including VAT. You collect it as Gala Dock's agent within the customer's payment; it isn't your income and isn't taken from your share (Payment Terms 2A).
5.5 **VAT.** Gala Dock invoices its commission to you. If you belong in the UK, commission carries UK VAT at the standard rate. If you belong outside the UK and give evidence you are in business, commission is generally outside the scope of UK VAT and you account for local VAT under the reverse charge [tax adviser: confirm, incl. land-related rules]. You invoice your own customers and account for VAT on your services. If you aren't VAT-registered, you must not charge VAT.
5.6 You're responsible for your own income tax, National Insurance and other taxes.
5.7 **Tax reporting.** UK law (the Platform Operators (Due Diligence and Reporting Requirements) Regulations 2023) requires us to collect and report information about suppliers and what they earn through Gala Dock: your name, address, date of birth (individuals) or company number, tax identification number and issuing country, VAT number and, where relevant, your bank account details as held by Stripe, and the amounts, number of bookings and fees per quarter. You must give accurate information when asked and keep it current. We report it to HMRC (which may share it with other tax authorities) and give you a copy. If you don't provide it after the reminders the Regulations require, payouts are held to the extent they require (Payment Terms 9.1(h)).
5.8 **Where you can be paid.** You can take payments through Gala Dock only if your business is established in a supported country (Terms §4A). Otherwise your listings are enquiry-only: customers can contact you, and any payment you agree with them is outside Gala Dock and not covered by the Payment Terms or the Disputes Policy. You must tell customers that clearly and must not suggest Gala Dock protection applies.

## 6. Cancellations, refunds and disputes
6.1 If you cancel a booking, the customer gets a full refund from your balance, including Gala Dock's admin fee (we return our commission), and the cancellation counts against your reliability score.
6.2 If the customer cancels, your saved cancellation policy and the Standard Booking Terms apply.
6.3 Disputes follow the Disputes Policy. Gala Dock only recommends. You must respond with evidence within the stated time.
6.4 Refunds and chargebacks after a payout are met as Payment Terms 6.4 says.

## 7. Reviews and reliability
Customers can review completed bookings; you may publish one response. You must not offer incentives for reviews, review yourself or competitors, or pressure customers. Your reliability score is calculated automatically from response time, acceptance, cancellations, disputes and delivery, and is explained in your workspace.

## 8. Keeping work on Gala Dock
8.1 For 12 months after a customer's first real contact with you through Gala Dock (a message, request, quote or booking), you must not invite or accept payment outside Gala Dock for an engagement that started on Gala Dock, unless you're enquiry-only (5.8), you can show you worked with that customer before, or we agree in writing.
8.2 If we reasonably believe this has happened, after telling you what we found and giving you 14 days to respond, we may charge a fee equal to the commission that would have been payable on the off-platform payments, once only, invoiced (never taken from your Stripe balance). Consumers are never charged. We may also restrict your account.
8.3 If a court finds this clause wider than reasonable, it applies to the extent that is reasonable.

## 9. Data
9.1 For customer data you receive through bookings, you and Gala Dock are each independent controllers. Use it only to deliver the booking and as the law allows, keep it secure, don't use it for unsolicited marketing, and report security incidents affecting it to the customer and to us without undue delay.
9.2 Where you collect data on behalf of an organiser through Gala Dock tools, the Data Processing Addendum applies.
9.3 Don't put customers' personal data into AI tools that would use it for training.

## 10. Suspension and closure
10.1 Terms §11 applies. We may hide listings or restrict features for: breach; failed or expired verification or insurance; credible safety, fraud or legal concerns; repeated cancellations or poor reliability; or requirements of Stripe or a regulator. A high chargeback or refund rate leads to a risk reserve under Payment Terms 5.2, not to suspension, unless it shows fraud or abuse.
10.2 Payouts are paused only on Payment Terms 9 grounds.
10.3 You may close your workspace after completing or cancelling outstanding bookings under their terms.

## 11. Responsibility
11.1 You are solely responsible and liable for your services, including quality, safety, legality, timing, staff, food safety and allergens, and for any injury, loss or damage they cause.
11.2 Our liability, the cap and your indemnity are in Terms of Service §§12–12A.

## 12. Verification and changes
12.1 Tell us within 14 days of changes to your details, ownership or control, address, tax status, insurance or bank. Don't use someone else's identity, a shell company or nominee account.
12.2 Stripe screens your account for sanctions under its own obligations. If it restricts your account, we send no payout instructions for it and tell you only what Stripe allows.

## 13. General
Terms of Service §§13–15 apply. Governing law: England and Wales; the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
