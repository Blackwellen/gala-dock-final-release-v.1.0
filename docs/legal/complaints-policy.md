# Complaints Policy

> **Draft for specialist UK legal review before production publication.** Version 2026-10.

We want to put things right when something goes wrong. This policy explains how to complain about Gala Dock and what happens next. It covers complaints about our platform, staff, payments, moderation and enforcement decisions, and data protection.

**Complaints about an event or a provider's service** should first go to the organiser or provider through Gala Dock messaging. If that doesn't resolve it, open a dispute from the booking (see the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms) or raise a complaint with us.

## How to complain

* In the app: Help → Contact support → Complaint
* Email: support@galadock.com (subject "Complaint")
* Post: Blackwellen Ltd, [REGISTERED ADDRESS]

Tell us what happened, when, the booking, order or ticket reference, and what you'd like us to do.

## What happens next

| Stage | Timescale |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Within 2 working days |
| Investigation and response | Within 10 working days; complex cases within 28 days, and we'll tell you if it will take longer and why |
| Review | If you're unhappy, ask for a review within 28 days. A senior team member not involved before reviews it and responds within 15 working days |

## Appeals against moderation and enforcement

If we removed content, restricted or suspended your account, hid a listing or template, or decided a dispute, you can appeal through the same process. The reviewer will:
* look at the original decision and any new information you provide;
* decide whether to uphold, change or reverse it;
* give written reasons.

## Data protection complaints

You can complain to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@galadock.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113), although we'd appreciate the chance to resolve it first.

## Alternative dispute resolution and courts

If we can't resolve a consumer complaint, you may be able to use an alternative dispute resolution scheme: **[ADR PROVIDER, if appointed]**. You can also go to court. Card payments may be protected by your card issuer's chargeback scheme or Section 75 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974.

## Record keeping

We keep complaint records for 3 years after closure to handle follow-up questions and improve our service.
