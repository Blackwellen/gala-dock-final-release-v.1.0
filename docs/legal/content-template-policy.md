# Content & Template Policy

> **Approved for publication by Jamahl Thomas, Director, Blackwellen Limited, 12 October 2026.** Version 2026-10. Covers Media Studio designs, uploads, event pages, listings and community templates.

## 1. Your content, your rights

1.1 You keep ownership of content you upload or create in Gala Dock: images, designs, text, menus, floor plans and templates.

1.2 You must have the rights to everything you use: your own work, licensed stock, or content you have permission to use. That includes photos of people (model consent where needed), logos and trade marks, artwork, fonts and music.

1.3 Gala Dock provides fonts and icons under open licences. You may use them in designs you create in Gala Dock, including for commercial event marketing. Uploaded custom fonts must be licensed for embedding and web use.

1.4 Gala Dock doesn't generate photographs of real people or places. If you use AI tools elsewhere, you're responsible for the output and for labelling it where that would avoid misleading people.

## 2. Private by default

Designs and uploads are private to you and your workspace unless you share or publish them. Private uploads are served only through short-lived, permission-checked links.

## 3. Publishing templates

3.1 You can save a design as a template that is private, for your workspace, for anyone with a link, or for the public community.

3.2 When you publish a template for link or public use, Gala Dock automatically:
* replaces event-specific details (event name, date, venue, prices, links and QR destinations) with placeholders such as `{{event.name}}`;
* removes images that come from your private uploads, unless you choose to publish a public copy of them.

Before publishing, check the template contains no personal data, confidential information or content you can't licence to others.

3.3 **Licence to other users.** By publishing a template for link or public use, you grant every Gala Dock user a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to copy, adapt and use the template in their own designs for their own events, for as long as it's published and indefinitely for copies already made. Users always get their own copy; your original isn't changed.

3.4 You can unpublish a template at any time. Copies already made remain with their users.

## 4. What isn't allowed

No content that:
* infringes copyright, trade marks, design rights or personality rights;
* is unlawful, hateful, sexually explicit, violent or harassing;
* is misleading, including fake endorsements, false sold-out claims or invented scarcity;
* contains malicious code, or QR codes or links to phishing or malware;
* imitates Gala Dock, another brand or a real person in a way that could deceive.

QR codes in designs must point to destinations you control or are authorised to use. Gala Dock warns about low-contrast or undersized codes that may not scan.

## 5. Reports and moderation

Anyone can report a public template. Three independent open reports hide it automatically pending review. Our moderators can hide, remove or reinstate templates, and can restrict creators who repeatedly breach this policy. Each decision records a reason. Copyright complaints follow the Intellectual Property & Takedown Procedure.
