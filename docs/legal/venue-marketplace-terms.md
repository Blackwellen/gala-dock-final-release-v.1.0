# Venue Marketplace Terms

> **Draft for specialist UK legal review before production publication.** Version 2026-10 · Additional terms for venues and space hire.

These terms add to the Provider Marketplace Terms for providers who list **venues and spaces** on Gala Dock. Where they conflict on a venue matter, these terms apply.

## 1. Listing a venue

1.1 You must have the right to hire out the venue and spaces you list, as owner, tenant or operator with authority, and you must have any consents your lease or landlord requires.

1.2 Your listing must accurately state:
* capacities for each layout (standing, seated, theatre, banquet and so on), which must not exceed your licensed or fire-risk-assessed capacity;
* access times, setup and breakdown windows, and curfews;
* noise limits;
* accessibility (step-free access, accessible toilets, hearing loops);
* power, rigging and load-in details;
* facilities, and what is included in the hire price.

1.3 Floor plans, venue maps and structural layouts you publish must reflect the real space. Event overlays created by organisers are their responsibility, but you must tell them about any constraint the overlay breaches (for example a blocked fire exit).

## 2. Licensing and safety

2.1 **Gala Dock does not hold any premises licence and does not authorise licensable activities.** You are responsible for: your premises licence or club premises certificate and its conditions; the Designated Premises Supervisor; temporary event notices; music and performance licensing; and compliance with fire safety, health and safety, and capacity limits.

2.2 Bar packages you sell must comply with your licence conditions, age-verification duties (Challenge 21/25), permitted hours, and any jurisdiction rules shown in Gala Dock. Gala Dock may block a package that breaches configured jurisdiction rules.

2.3 You must share relevant safety information with organisers: fire risk assessment constraints, evacuation routes, stewarding ratios, and any conditions on special effects, pyrotechnics or naked flames.

## 3. Rate cards, holds and conflicts

3.1 Prices are generated from your rate cards: day parts, minimum spend, deposits, seasonal and day-of-week rules, and add-ons. The quote shown to the customer is binding once accepted.

3.2 Holds reserve a space for the stated period. Gala Dock detects calendar conflicts across your spaces (including linked and combinable spaces) and won't let you double-confirm a space. You remain responsible for bookings you take outside Gala Dock and must block them in your calendar.

3.3 If you can't honour a confirmed booking, you must tell the customer and Gala Dock immediately and may offer alternatives. The customer is entitled to a full refund if they don't accept an alternative.

## 4. Event orders (BEOs), catering and menus

Banquet event orders and catering selections are versioned in Gala Dock. Once approved by both parties, an event order forms part of the booking. Changes after approval need a new version and, where prices change, the customer's confirmation. Menus must carry accurate allergen information covering the 14 major allergens, and dietary labels must be truthful.

## 5. Damage, deposits and house rules

Your house rules and any security or damage deposit must be disclosed in the quote. Damage claims must be raised within 7 days after the event, with evidence, through Gala Dock. Undisputed claims may be deducted from a damage deposit you hold. Disputed claims follow the dispute process.

## 6. Payment protection

Venue hire payments are protected and released under the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms (after the event, subject to disputes). Long-lead bookings may be released in stages where agreed in the quote and permitted by our payment processor.
