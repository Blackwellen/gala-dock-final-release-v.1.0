# Cancellation & Refund Policy

> **Draft for specialist UK legal review before production publication.** Version 2026-10.

This policy explains what happens to your money when a ticket or booking is cancelled, changed or goes wrong. It applies to customers, organisers and providers. It doesn't remove any rights you have by law.

## 1. Who sets the rules

* **Tickets:** the organiser's refund policy, shown on the event page and at checkout, plus this policy.
* **Provider bookings** (venues, catering, entertainment, equipment, staff): the provider's **cancellation policy**, which is attached to the quote you accept and recorded with the booking. Later changes to the provider's policy don't affect your booking.
* **Gala Dock fees and subscriptions:** section 6.

## 2. Ticket refunds

| What happened | What you get |
|---|---|
| Organiser cancels the event | Full refund of the ticket price and booking fee, to the original payment method |
| Event moved to a new date, time or venue | You can keep your ticket. If the change is material (for example a different date or a significantly different venue or headliner), you can ask for a refund within the period the organiser announces, which must be at least 14 days |
| You can't attend | No automatic refund. You can **transfer** the ticket to someone else, or **resell** it through Gala Dock where allowed, and receive what the buyer pays (up to your original price) |
| Event happened but was significantly different from what was advertised | Contact the organiser through Gala Dock. If it isn't resolved, raise a complaint with us; we'll review it with the organiser |

Refunds go back to the original payment method, normally within 5–10 working days of being approved (your bank may take longer to show them).

## 3. Provider booking cancellations

### 3.1 If you (the customer or organiser) cancel

Gala Dock calculates your refund using the provider's policy for the booking. A policy is a set of tiers such as "60+ days before the event: 100% refund", "30–59 days: 50%", "under 30 days: 0%". We use the number of days between your cancellation and the event start. The screen shows you the exact amount **before** you confirm.

* Deposits and amounts marked non-refundable in the quote are only kept if the quote said so clearly before you accepted it.
* If the booking has **no recorded policy**, we don't apply an automatic forfeit. The cancellation goes to Gala Dock for review.

### 3.2 If the provider cancels

You receive a **full refund** of everything paid for that booking, and the cancellation counts against the provider's reliability record. Where available we'll show suitable replacements, including any backup or standby providers already lined up for your event.

### 3.3 Rescheduling

A reschedule keeps the booking value and needs the provider's agreement to the new date. If the provider can't do the new date, the booking may be cancelled under 3.1 or 3.2 depending on who can't proceed.

### 3.4 Events that can't go ahead (force majeure)

If an event can't lawfully or safely take place for reasons outside either party's control (for example a government restriction or severe weather warning that closes the venue), the cancellation is **reviewed by Gala Dock**. Our starting point is a refund of amounts paid for services not delivered, less reasonable costs the provider has actually and unavoidably incurred and can evidence.

### 3.5 How refunds are paid

Refunds for bookings are queued as soon as a cancellation is accepted and paid back to the original payment method. If part of the payment has already been released to the provider, we recover the provider's share in proportion and refund you.

## 4. Problems with a delivered service

If a provider doesn't turn up, delivers something materially different from the booking, or delivers it without reasonable care and skill, open a **dispute** from the booking within **48 hours after the event ends**. While the dispute is open, the payment is not released to the provider. See the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms for how disputes are decided.

## 5. Chargebacks

Please contact us before asking your bank for a chargeback; we can usually resolve things faster. If a chargeback is opened, the related payment stays protected until it is resolved, and we submit the booking evidence to the card network.

## 6. Gala Dock fees, plans and credits

* **Ticket booking fees** (£1.00 per ticket) are refunded to buyers when the organiser cancels the event, and otherwise when the law requires it.
* **Organiser cancellations:** buyers are refunded in full, but the organiser still owes Gala Dock the platform fees on the tickets sold (the 5% seller commission and the booking fees refunded to buyers). These are deducted from the organiser's proceeds or payouts, or invoiced.
* **Booking admin fee** on venue and supplier bookings (2.5%, minimum £1) is refunded if the provider or Gala Dock cancels, and is otherwise not refundable.
* **Provider commission** is only earned on completed bookings. If a booking is fully refunded, no commission is kept.
* **Subscriptions:** you can cancel a paid plan at any time from Settings → Payments & Billing. It stays active until the end of the paid period. We don't refund part-periods unless the law requires it or we have failed to provide the service.
* **AI credit packs:** credits are a digital service that starts as soon as you buy them, with your consent. Unused credits expire after 12 months. Credits wrongly consumed because of a fault on our side are restored.

Use Help in the app or email support@galadock.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled a refund, see the Complaints Policy.
