# Provider Marketplace Terms

> **Approved for publication by Jamahl Thomas, Director, Blackwellen Limited, 12 October 2026.** Version 2026-10 · Business terms for suppliers (catering, entertainment, AV, staffing, equipment, décor, transport and other services).

These terms apply when you list services and take bookings through Gala Dock as a **Provider**. Venues are additionally covered by the Venue Marketplace Terms. These terms incorporate the Platform Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, Community & Marketplace Standards, Marketplace Payment Protection Terms, Cancellation & Refund Policy and AI / Gala Agent Terms.

Contracting entity: Blackwellen Limited (company number 16482166), registered office 61 Bridge Street, Kington, Herefordshire, HR5 3DJ.

## 1. Our relationship

1.1 **You use our software; you don't work for us.** Gala Dock provides software that lets you list services, receive requests, quote, take bookings and get paid. You are an independent business using that software under these terms. You are not Gala Dock's employee, worker, partner, joint venturer or subcontractor, Gala Dock does not supply your services to anyone, and nothing in these terms creates employment or a contract of service.

1.2 **You supply your services.** Your contract for each booking is directly between you and the customer or organiser. You decide how, when and by whom your services are performed, and you are responsible for your own staff, equipment, tax, National Insurance and insurance. Gala Dock does not supervise or direct your work.

1.3 **Payment collection only.** So that customers can pay through the platform, you appoint Gala Dock as your **limited, non-exclusive agent to collect payments** for your bookings on your behalf. Payment to Gala Dock discharges the customer's obligation to pay you. This appointment is limited to taking and holding payments and doesn't make Gala Dock the supplier of your services.

## 2. Listings

2.1 Listings must be accurate and current: what's included, prices, minimums, service area, capacity, lead times, dietary and allergen information (for food), certifications, insurance and availability. Photos must show your actual work or products, or be clearly labelled as illustrative.

2.2 You must keep your calendar and availability up to date. Accepting a booking you can't deliver is a serious breach.

2.3 We may verify your identity, business details, insurance and qualifications before your listings go live, and from time to time afterwards (see "Verification"). Verification badges reflect the checks we have done at a point in time; they are not a guarantee.

## 3. Requests, quotes, holds and standby

3.1 **Respond on time.** Requests show a response deadline. Your response rate and time feed your reliability score, which affects search placement.

3.2 **Quotes** are binding offers for their validity period. They must include all compulsory charges, the deposit and balance schedule, and your cancellation policy. Your cancellation policy must be fair and transparent. Gala Dock may refuse policies that would be unfair to consumers.

3.3 **Conditional holds** reserve capacity until their expiry. You must honour a hold until it expires or is released.

3.4 **Standby** commitments are paid commitments to be available as a backup. If a standby is activated within its terms, you must deliver as if booked.

3.5 **Emergency requests** have short response windows. Accepting one is a commitment to deliver at the stated time.

## 4. Delivery

4.1 You must deliver services as booked, with reasonable care and skill, on time, and in compliance with law: food hygiene and allergen law, health and safety, licensing, employment and right-to-work law, and insurance.

4.2 You must use the booking's check-in tools where required (for example arrival and setup status on event day) and tell the organiser promptly about delays or problems.

4.3 You must hold appropriate insurance, normally public liability of at least £2,000,000 per occurrence (venues may require more), and provide evidence on request.

## 5. Payment, commission and payouts

5.1 Customers pay through Gala Dock under section 1.3.

5.2 Funds are held under the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms and **released to your connected payout account after delivery** (fulfilled/completed, or 48 hours after the event ends), provided there is no open dispute, chargeback or pending cancellation and your payout account is in good standing.

5.3 **Commission** is a platform usage fee for using the Gala Dock software and payment tools. It is deducted from each payment at the rate recorded on the booking when the payment was made: **8% on the Starter plan, 7% on Pro and 6% on Business or Enterprise**, unless we have agreed a different rate with you in writing. Commission is not charged on amounts refunded to the customer.

5.3A **Customer admin fee.** Customers pay Gala Dock a separate booking admin fee of 2.5% of each payment (minimum £1). It is Gala Dock's fee, is shown to the customer before they pay, and is not deducted from your share.

5.4 You must complete payout onboarding with our payment processor (Stripe) and keep it in good standing. If you don't, funds stay protected until you do.

5.5 You're responsible for your own taxes, including VAT on your services. Gala Dock accounts for VAT on its commission and fees. [Tax adviser: confirm the invoicing model — self-billing or provider invoices — and VAT treatment.]

## 6. Cancellations, refunds and disputes

6.1 If you cancel a booking, the customer receives a full refund, the cancellation counts against your reliability score, and repeated cancellations may lead to restriction.

6.2 If the customer cancels, your recorded cancellation policy determines the refund.

6.3 Disputes are decided under the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms. You must respond to a dispute and provide evidence within the stated deadline. Our decision about money held by Gala Dock is final as between the parties for that money, but doesn't prevent either party bringing a claim.

6.4 If a refund or chargeback is due after funds were released to you, we may reverse transfers or set the amount off against future payouts. You must repay any shortfall within 14 days of our request.

## 7. Reviews and reliability

Customers can review completed bookings. You can publish a response. You must not offer incentives for reviews, review your own services, or pressure customers to change reviews. Your reliability score (response time, acceptance, cancellations, disputes and delivery) is calculated automatically and explained in your workspace.

## 8. Non-circumvention

For 12 months after a customer first contacts you through Gala Dock, you must not invite or accept payment outside Gala Dock for engagements that originated on Gala Dock, unless we agree in writing.

## 9. Data

For customer data you receive through bookings, you and Gala Dock are each independent controllers for your own purposes. You must use customer data only to deliver the booking and as the law allows, keep it secure, and not use it for unsolicited marketing.

## 10. Suspension, termination and liability

10.1 We may suspend listings or payouts, with notice where practicable, for:
* material breach;
* failed or expired verification;
* credible safety, fraud or legal concerns;
* repeated cancellations or poor reliability;
* requirements of our payment processor or a regulator.

10.2 You may close your workspace at any time after completing or cancelling outstanding bookings under their terms.

10.3 **Responsibility for your services.** You are solely responsible and liable for your services and venue, including their quality, safety, legality, timing, staff, food safety and allergens, and for any injury, loss or damage they cause. Gala Dock is not liable for your acts or omissions or those of your staff and subcontractors.

10.4 **Indemnity.** You indemnify Gala Dock against losses, claims, fines, refunds and chargebacks (including reasonable legal costs) arising from your services, your breach of these terms or of law, or claims by customers, attendees or regulators about your services, except to the extent caused by Gala Dock's breach or negligence.

10.5 **Our liability.** Nothing limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or anything that can't be limited by law. Otherwise, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss or loss of profit, revenue, goodwill or data, and Gala Dock's total liability to you in any 12-month period is limited to the greater of (a) the commission and fees you paid to Gala Dock in that period and (b) £1,000. Booking funds held for you, which remain payable to you, are not counted against this cap.

10.6 Governing law: England and Wales.
