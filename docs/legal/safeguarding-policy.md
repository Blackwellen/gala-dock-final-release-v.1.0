# Safeguarding & Abuse Policy

> **Approved for publication by Jamahl Thomas, Director, Blackwellen Limited, 12 October 2026.** Version 2026-10. Safeguarding lead: **Jamahl Thomas, Director**; deputy: **not yet appointed — the lead covers all reports until a deputy is named**.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Gala Dock is used to plan events that children and adults at risk may attend, such as children's parties, school events and community festivals, and to message between customers, organisers and providers. This policy sets out how Blackwellen Limited prevents, recognises and responds to abuse, exploitation and harm connected with the platform. It applies to our staff, contractors and administrators, and sets expectations for every user.

Gala Dock does not run events, employ event staff for organisers, or carry out regulated activity with children or adults at risk. Organisers and providers remain responsible for safeguarding at their events, including DBS checks where the law requires them.

## 2. Principles

* The welfare of children and adults at risk is paramount.
* Everyone has a right to protection from abuse, whatever their age, disability, gender, race, religion or belief, sexual orientation or identity.
* We act on concerns promptly, share information only where necessary and lawful, and keep proper records.

## 3. What we expect of users

* Organisers and providers working with children or adults at risk must follow their own safeguarding policies and legal duties, including safer recruitment and DBS checks where applicable.
* Don't use Gala Dock messaging to contact children directly. Customer accounts are for adults (18+).
* Report concerns immediately: use **Report** in the app or email support@galadock.com. **If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.**

## 4. How we respond to a concern

1. **Receive.** Reports go to the trust and safety queue, and safeguarding reports are prioritised. The person reviewing records what was reported, when and by whom.
2. **Assess.** The safeguarding lead (or deputy) decides whether there is a risk of significant harm. If there is an immediate risk, we contact the police (999) or the local authority's children's or adult services. We don't investigate crimes ourselves.
3. **Protect.** We may suspend accounts, hide listings or events, restrict messaging and preserve evidence.
4. **Access to messages.** Gala Dock staff cannot browse private conversations. Where reviewing messages is necessary for a safeguarding case, a trained administrator requests **break-glass access** linked to the case. This needs a written justification, approval by a second administrator for safeguarding cases, and step-up verification. It is time-limited and read-only, and every conversation opened is logged. Details are in the Privacy Notice and the break-glass DPIA (`docs/compliance/dpia-break-glass-access.md`).
5. **Refer and share.** We share information with the police, local authority designated officers (LADO) and other agencies where the law requires or permits it (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)/(d)/(e); DPA 2018 Sch.1 para. 18 safeguarding of children and individuals at risk).
6. **Record and review.** Every case is recorded with its decisions and reasons. Records are kept securely and retained under the Data Retention Schedule. The safeguarding lead reviews case handling quarterly.

## 5. Notifying affected users

Where it's lawful and safe, we tell users after an investigation closes if their messages were accessed. We don't notify them where it could put someone at risk or prejudice a criminal investigation, and we record the reason.

## 6. Staff

Staff and administrators with access to trust and safety tools complete safeguarding and data protection training at induction and every year. Admin access needs MFA, and break-glass access is reviewed by the DPO.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed every year, or sooner after a serious case or a change in law.
