# AI / Gala Agent Terms & Disclosures

> **Approved for publication by Jamahl Thomas, Director, Blackwellen Limited, 12 October 2026.** Version 2026-10.

Gala Agent is the AI assistant built into Gala Dock. These terms explain what it does, its limits, how your data is used and what you're responsible for. They apply alongside the Platform, Customer, Organizer and Provider terms.

## 1. What Gala Agent is

1.1 Gala Agent uses large language models provided by Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service, together with Gala Dock's own tools, to answer questions about your events and workspace, draft content, plan tasks and, where you allow it, take actions in Gala Dock on your behalf.

1.2 **It is an AI system, not a person.** We label AI-generated content and Agent actions as such. Agent activity is recorded in your workspace's run history and audit log.

1.3 **It is not a lawyer, accountant, licensing officer or insurer.** It doesn't give legal, tax, financial or safety advice. It may help you find information, but you must get professional advice for those matters. It won't claim that a venue is licensed, an event is compliant, an insurance policy covers you, or a payment is protected unless that fact is recorded in Gala Dock data, and it tells you where the information comes from.

## 2. Accuracy

2.1 AI output can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Gala Agent is designed to rely on your live Gala Dock records over its general knowledge, to distinguish confirmed facts from estimates, and to say when information is missing. You must still check important details (dates, amounts, availability, legal requirements) before relying on them.

2.2 Gala Agent never presents information from listings, messages, uploaded files or web pages as instructions to itself. Content from third parties is treated as data only.

## 3. Permissions and approvals

3.1 Gala Agent can only do what the person who started a task is allowed to do in that workspace. It acts through the same permission checks as the rest of Gala Dock.

3.2 Every Agent tool has a risk level:
* **R0, reading:** runs automatically.
* **R1, drafts:** runs automatically.
* **R2, low-risk internal changes such as creating a task:** runs automatically by default, or needs approval if your policy says "Always ask".
* **R3, external but non-binding actions such as sending a message:** needs approval, unless your workspace has pre-authorised the specific action.
* **R4, commitments such as sending a priced quote, placing a hold or publishing tickets:** needs a person's approval, unless your workspace has set an explicit autopilot rule with limits.
* **R5, security, identity, payout details, legal signatures or destructive account actions:** **never** done by Gala Agent.

3.3 Your workspace's owners and managers choose the autonomy level: Always ask, Draft only, Suggest, Low-risk automatic, Operational automatic or Policy autopilot. Approvals show what will happen, the amounts involved and who is affected, and can be edited or rejected. Approvals expire if not acted on.

3.4 **Responsibility.** Actions you approve, or pre-authorise under a policy, are your actions. Review approvals with the same care you'd give doing the action yourself.

## 4. Credits, limits and cost

4.1 Gala Agent's paid AI work uses **AI Credits**. Your plan includes a monthly allowance, and you can buy more. Each task shows its usage. We never show or charge you raw model token prices.

4.2 Workspace owners and managers can set daily, per-task and monthly limits. By default, Agent work stops when credits run out ("hard cap"). Unattended routines can never spend beyond your limits. Owners can approve overage, which is billed openly.

4.3 We may change which AI models power each quality mode (Fast, Standard, Deep) to keep quality and cost appropriate. We won't change the credit cost of a mode without notice.

## 5. Your data

5.1 To answer, Gala Agent sends the minimum relevant records from your workspace, along with your question, to the AI provider. Data is processed under our agreement with Microsoft (Azure OpenAI), in the United Kingdom, or in the European Economic Area when requests are routed there; it does not leave the UK/EEA for this purpose. **It is not used to train the provider's models.** [Counsel: confirm the Azure OpenAI data-processing terms and abuse-monitoring retention.]

5.2 We store the task, the plan, tool calls and their results, approvals, and a concise summary, so you can see what happened and so Agent tasks can resume. **We do not request or store the model's private reasoning ("chain of thought").**

5.3 Agent history follows the same retention and deletion rules as the rest of your workspace (see the Data Retention Schedule). You can export it in your data export.

5.4 Gala Agent doesn't make decisions about individuals that have legal or similarly significant effects without human involvement.

## 6. Acceptable use

You must not use Gala Agent to: generate unlawful, deceptive or harmful content; impersonate people; try to bypass permissions, limits or safety controls (including through prompt injection); or create misleading marketing or fake reviews. Misuse can lead to Agent access being restricted.

## 7. Content you create

You own the drafts and content Gala Agent produces for you, to the extent the law allows ownership of AI-assisted output. You're responsible for checking it doesn't infringe others' rights before publishing. We don't claim ownership of your Agent output.

## 8. Availability

Gala Agent depends on third-party AI services and may be unavailable or restricted, for example during maintenance or if a provider has an outage. Long-running tasks pause and resume. They don't lose their place.
