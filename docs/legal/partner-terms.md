# Affiliate & Partner Terms

> **Draft for specialist UK legal review before production publication.** Version 2026-10.

These terms apply to organisations that join the Gala Dock **Partner** programme (for example agencies, venues groups, associations and integrators) to refer customers, resell plans, or build integrations, and to their use of the Partner portal. Individual event promoters are covered by the Promoter Terms instead.

## 1. Programme

1.1 Partners are approved by Gala Dock. Approval can be withdrawn under section 7.

1.2 Partner types:
* **referral partners**, who earn a fee for referred workspaces that become paying customers;
* **solutions partners**, who set up and manage workspaces for clients with the client's authority;
* **technology partners**, who build integrations using the Gala Dock API, MCP server and webhooks.

## 2. Referral fees

2.1 A referral counts when a new workspace signs up through the partner's tracked link or code and starts a paid plan within 60 days. Fees, the payment period and any caps are set individually in each partner’s signed order form and shown in the partner portal.

2.2 No fees are due on refunded amounts, on fraudulent or self-referred accounts, or on existing customers.

## 3. Acting for clients

Solutions partners may only access a client's workspace as a member invited by that client, with the role the client assigns. The client controls access and can remove it at any time. The partner must follow the client's instructions and the Data Processing Addendum obligations that flow down to them.

## 4. Technology partners and the API

Use of the API and MCP server is subject to the API key rules in the Organizer and Provider terms:
* keys act with the permissions of the person who created them;
* keys are limited to their scopes;
* commitments always need approval in the app;
* rate limits apply.

Partners must store keys and webhook secrets securely, verify webhook signatures, and not resell API access.

## 5. Brand and marketing

Partners may describe themselves as a "Gala Dock Partner" and use the logos we provide, following our brand guidelines. They must not suggest they are Gala Dock or make commitments on our behalf.

## 6. Confidentiality and data

Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential. Partners receive aggregated referral data only, unless a client authorises more.

## 7. Term and termination

Either party may end the programme relationship on 30 days' notice, or immediately for material breach. Earned and payable fees survive. Liability: each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid or payable under these terms in the previous 12 months, except for liability that cannot be limited by law.

Governing law: England and Wales.
