# Intellectual Property & Takedown Procedure

> **Approved for publication by Jamahl Thomas, Director, Blackwellen Limited, 12 October 2026.** Version 2026-10.

Gala Dock hosts content uploaded by users: event pages, listings, images, designs, templates and reviews. We respect intellectual property rights and act quickly on valid notices, in line with the Electronic Commerce (EC Directive) Regulations 2002.

## 1. Reporting infringement

Send a notice to legal@galadock.com, or use **Report → Intellectual property** on the content. Include:
1. your name and contact details, and the rights holder's if you act for them;
2. the work or right you say is infringed: copyright work, registered or unregistered trade mark, or design;
3. the exact location of the content (URL or template link);
4. why you believe it infringes;
5. a statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.

## 2. What we do

* We review notices promptly, normally within 2 working days.
* If the notice is complete and the content appears to infringe, we remove or disable access to it, and tell the uploader, giving them the notice details (we may withhold your personal contact details).
* Public templates may be hidden immediately while we review.

## 3. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, or that you have the right to use it, reply to our email with your explanation and evidence (for example a licence). We may restore the content if the counter-notice is credible, after telling the complainant, unless they show they have started legal proceedings.

## 4. Repeat infringers

Accounts that receive repeated valid notices may have publishing restricted, or be suspended or terminated.

## 5. Misuse

Knowingly false notices may be unlawful, and we may decline to act on notices from repeat abusers of this process.

## 6. Gala Dock's own IP

"Gala Dock", our logos and our platform design are our property. Don't use them in a way that suggests endorsement without our written permission.
