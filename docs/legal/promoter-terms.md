# Promoter Terms

> **Draft for specialist UK legal review before production publication.** Version 2026-10.

These terms apply to people and businesses who promote events on Gala Dock using **promoter links** in the Promoter portal, and to the organisers who invite them. They sit alongside the Platform Terms and the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 1. How promotion works

1.1 An organiser invites you to promote an event and sets the commission: a percentage or fixed amount per paid ticket, which the portal shows.

1.2 You share your personal promoter link (`galadock.com/r/<code>`). When someone follows it, we count the click and remember the referral so a ticket purchase for that event within **30 days** is credited to you.

1.3 Credit is given only for completed, paid primary-ticket orders for that event. Refunded, cancelled or charged-back orders are reversed. Resale purchases and free tickets don't earn commission.

## 2. Payment

2.1 The organiser owes your commission. Gala Dock shows your attributed sales and earnings in the portal and, where the organiser has enabled payouts through Gala Dock, pays commission from the organiser's proceeds after the event has taken place and refund risk has passed.

2.2 You're responsible for your own tax.

## 3. Rules for promoters

* Promote honestly. Don't misstate prices, line-ups, availability or scarcity, and don't claim to be the organiser.
* **Disclose that you're paid.** Social posts must say so clearly, for example with "#ad", following CAP Code and ASA guidance.
* No spam, unsolicited direct messages, fake accounts, cookie stuffing, self-referrals, or buying tickets through your own link to earn commission.
* Comply with the platform's advertising rules for paid ads; don't bid on "Gala Dock" brand terms.
* Only promote events you've been invited to.

## 4. Privacy and cookies

The referral is recorded using a first-party cookie (`gd_ref`) and a privacy-preserving visitor hash. We don't give promoters buyers' personal data, only aggregated counts and amounts. **[Counsel: confirm whether the attribution cookie requires consent under PECR; if so, attribution will only be set after consent.]**

## 5. Ending

Organisers can end a promotion at any time. Commission already earned on qualifying orders remains payable. We may suspend promoters who break these terms and reverse commission obtained through breaches.
