## 1. What we do, and what we don't

1.1 Gala Dock is a marketplace and planning platform. We let you find and buy tickets for events run by **organisers**, and find, request quotes from and book **providers** (venues, caterers, entertainers, equipment and other suppliers).

1.2 **We are not the organiser of events listed by others, and we are not the provider of venues or services booked through Gala Dock.** Organisers and providers are independent businesses that use the Gala Dock software; they don't work for us. The organiser is responsible, and liable, for its event; the provider is responsible, and liable, for the venue or service it agrees to deliver. When you buy a ticket or book a provider, your contract for the event or service is with that organiser or provider. We act as their commercial agent to take your payment and, for bookings, hold it under our payment protection arrangement (see the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms).

1.3 We are responsible to you for providing the Gala Dock platform itself with reasonable care and skill, for handling your payments as described, and for the accuracy of information we write ourselves. We are not responsible for statements made by organisers or providers in their listings, but we take action on misleading listings (see the Community & Marketplace Standards).

## 2. Your account

2.1 You must be 18 or over to create an account, buy tickets or make bookings. Some events have higher age limits set by the organiser; you may be asked for ID at the door.

2.2 Keep your sign-in details secure. We strongly recommend turning on two-step verification (Account → Privacy & Security). Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

2.3 You can close your account at any time from Account → Privacy & Security. Closing your account starts a 14-day grace period, after which your personal data is deleted or anonymised as described in the Privacy Notice and Data Retention Schedule. We will tell you if something blocks deletion (for example tickets for an upcoming event or an open dispute).

## 3. Prices and what you pay

3.1 The price you see before you pay includes all compulsory charges, including booking fees, so you won't be asked to pay unavoidable extras later. Optional extras are shown separately and only added if you choose them.

3.2 For tickets, the organiser sets the face value. Gala Dock charges a booking fee of £1.00 per paid ticket, a platform usage fee shown before you pay. Free tickets carry no fee.

3.3 For provider bookings, the provider sets its prices and may ask for a deposit with the balance due later. The quote shows the deposit, balance, due dates and the provider's cancellation policy before you accept it. Gala Dock adds a booking admin fee of 2.5% of each payment (minimum £1), a platform usage fee shown as its own line before you pay. It is refunded if the provider cancels.

3.4 Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

3.5 **A payment is only confirmed when the payment provider confirms it to us.** If you leave the payment page early or your connection drops, check Account → Payments; we never take a payment twice for the same order.

## 4. Requests, quotes, holds and bookings

4.1 When you request a quote, the provider may reply with a quote, decline, or suggest alternatives. A quote is an offer by the provider. **A booking is formed only when you accept the quote and pay any deposit required** (or the provider confirms, where no deposit is required).

4.2 A provider may offer a **conditional hold** or **standby** arrangement. These are explained on screen with their expiry time. A hold expires automatically if it isn't confirmed in time.

4.3 You must give accurate event details: date, times, guest numbers, venue access, dietary requirements and allergens. The provider relies on them. If you change details after booking, the provider may need to re-quote.

## 5. Your rights to cancel

5.1 **Tickets and bookings for a specific date are leisure services, so the general 14-day right to cancel online purchases does not apply** (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, reg. 28(1)(b)). Your cancellation rights are set by the organiser's or provider's policy, which is shown before you pay, and by the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

5.2 If the **organiser cancels** an event, or a **provider cancels** your booking, you get a full refund of what you paid for that ticket or booking.

5.3 If you buy **AI credits** or another digital service, you'll be asked to agree that it starts immediately. Once it starts, you lose the 14-day right to cancel for any part already supplied.

## 6. Payment protection and problems with a booking

6.1 When you pay a provider through Gala Dock, the money is held under our payment protection arrangement and released to the provider only after the service is delivered (see the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms). This gives you time to raise a problem.

6.2 If something goes wrong, first message the provider through Gala Dock. If you can't resolve it, open a **dispute** from the booking page within **48 hours after the event ends**. While a dispute is open, payment is not released to the provider.

6.3 We review disputes fairly, using the booking record, messages, photos and other evidence both sides provide, and we give reasons for our decision. This doesn't affect your legal rights, including your right to go to court.

## 7. Tickets

7.1 Tickets are personal to the event and governed by the Ticket Purchase Terms. Your ticket QR code changes every 30 seconds in the app to prevent screenshots being reused. You can also print a ticket. Each ticket is valid for one entry unless the organiser says otherwise.

7.2 You can transfer a ticket to a friend or resell it through **Gala Dock's official resale** where the organiser allows it. Resale is at no more than you paid (see the Official Ticket Resale Terms). Selling Gala Dock tickets elsewhere may make the ticket invalid if the organiser's terms prohibit it.

## 8. Reviews and content you post

8.1 You can review a provider or event you booked or attended. Reviews must be honest and about your own genuine experience. We don't allow paid, incentivised or fake reviews, and we may remove reviews that break the Community & Marketplace Standards. Providers may publish a response.

8.2 You keep ownership of what you upload. You give us a licence to host and display it to run Gala Dock, as set out in the Platform Terms.

Gala Agent is an AI assistant. It can make mistakes, so check important details yourself. It cannot pay, book or cancel anything for you without your confirmation. See the AI / Gala Agent Terms & Disclosures.
