# Organizer Terms

> **Draft for specialist UK legal review before production publication.** Version 2026-10 · Business terms for organisers (B2B).

These terms apply to businesses and individuals acting in the course of business who use Gala Dock's **Organizer** workspace to plan events, sell tickets, source venues and suppliers, manage teams and run event operations ("**you**", the "**Organizer**"). They incorporate the Platform Terms of Service, the Acceptable Use Policy, the Community & Marketplace Standards, the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms, the AI / Gala Agent Terms, and, where you process attendee data through Gala Dock, the Data Processing Addendum.

The contracting entity is Blackwellen Limited (company number 16482166), registered office 61 Bridge Street, Kington, HR5 3DJ ("**Gala Dock**", "**we**").

## 1. Your workspace

1.1 **Accounts and roles.** You create a workspace and invite members with roles (owner, manager, member, finance). The owner is responsible for who has access and what roles they hold. Gala Dock enforces role permissions on every action, including actions taken through the API, MCP and Gala Agent.

1.2 **Security.** You must keep credentials secure. You can require two-step verification for some or all members (Settings → Security). We recommend requiring it for owners, managers and finance roles. You are responsible for activity under your workspace's credentials and API keys until you tell us they've been compromised and we've had a reasonable opportunity to act.

1.3 **Plans and entitlements.** Your plan sets limits such as active events, team size, storage, AI credits, API access and advanced reporting. Limits are shown in Settings → Payments & Billing and enforced by the platform. Exceeding a limit pauses the relevant capability; it never deletes your data.

## 2. Selling tickets

2.1 **You are the seller.** When attendees buy tickets for your event, the contract for attending the event is between you and the attendee. Gala Dock acts as your commercial agent to sell tickets, take payment and issue credentials.

2.2 **Your obligations to attendees.** You must:
* describe the event accurately, including date, time, venue, age limits, accessibility and restrictions;
* set out your refund policy before sale, and comply with it and with consumer law;
* run the event as advertised, with reasonable care and skill;
* hold any licences, permissions and insurance the event requires (including premises and alcohol licensing, temporary event notices, music licensing, safety certificates and capacity limits).

2.3 **Pricing.** You set face values. The price shown to buyers includes all compulsory fees. You choose whether booking fees are absorbed or passed on. Gala Dock's booking fee is set out in your plan and at checkout.

2.4 **Cancellation and changes.** If you cancel an event, you must cancel it through Gala Dock, which voids all tickets and refunds every buyer in full, including fees, from the ticket proceeds. You can't cancel an event with paid orders in any other way. If you materially change an event, you must offer refunds as required by the Cancellation & Refund Policy. You indemnify Gala Dock against refunds, chargebacks and claims arising from your event, cancellation or failure to deliver, except to the extent caused by our breach.

2.5 **Settlement.** Ticket proceeds, less fees, refunds and chargebacks, are paid to your connected payout account under your settlement schedule. We may hold proceeds for an event until it has taken place where there is a material risk of refunds (for example a new organiser, a large event or a pattern of complaints). We'll tell you if we do.

2.6 **Resale and transfers.** You decide whether attendees can transfer or resell tickets, and you can set a resale cap at or below face value. Resale through Gala Dock always issues the buyer a new credential.

2.7 **Check-in.** You're responsible for door staff and for checking ID where needed. The scanner verifies credentials and records check-ins; you must follow its result (for example "already used" or "wrong event") unless you have a good reason to override it, and overrides are logged.

## 3. Buying from providers through the marketplace

3.1 When you request quotes, hold, book or pay providers through Gala Dock, the Marketplace Payment Protection Terms apply: money is protected until delivery, then released to the provider.

3.2 You must give providers accurate event information (dates, times, access, power, guest numbers, dietary and allergen needs, and safety requirements) and pay on the agreed schedule.

3.3 Cancellations of bookings follow the provider's cancellation policy recorded on the booking, and the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

3.4 **No off-platform circumvention.** For 12 months after you first contact a provider through Gala Dock, you must not arrange to pay that provider outside Gala Dock for an engagement that started on Gala Dock without our written consent. Off-platform payments aren't protected and break the Community & Marketplace Standards.

## 4. Attendee and client data

4.1 For attendee and guest data you collect through your events (ticket buyers' names, emails and answers to buyer questions, guest lists, crew details), **you are the controller and Gala Dock is your processor** under the Data Processing Addendum, except where Gala Dock processes the same data for its own purposes (for example fraud prevention, payments, legal compliance and operating customer accounts), when Gala Dock is an independent controller as described in the Privacy Notice.

4.2 You must have a lawful basis for collecting and using attendee data, and give attendees any privacy information required. You must only send marketing messages where you have consent or another lawful basis under PECR.

You confirm you have the rights to everything you upload. See the Content & Template Policy.

6.1 Subscription fees are billed in advance, monthly or annually, and are non-refundable except as required by law or where we fail to provide the service. Booking fees and commission are deducted from proceeds or invoiced.

6.2 Prices exclude VAT, which is added where applicable.

6.3 We may change our fees with at least 30 days' notice. Changes don't apply to tickets already sold or bookings already made.

## 7. Gala Agent and automation

Gala Agent acts only within the permissions of the person who started the task and your workspace's Agent policy. Consequential actions (commitments, payments and anything external) require approval unless you've expressly pre-authorised them within limits. You're responsible for reviewing drafts and approvals. See the AI / Gala Agent Terms.

8.2 You may close your workspace at any time. Closing doesn't cancel live events. You must cancel and refund or complete them first. Financial records are kept for legal retention periods.

9.4 Gala Dock is not responsible for the delivery of providers' services, for your event, or for attendees' conduct.
