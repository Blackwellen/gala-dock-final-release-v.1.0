# Marketplace Payment Protection Terms

> **Draft for specialist UK legal review before production publication.** Version 2026-10. **This is not an escrow service and must never be described as escrow.**

These terms explain how Gala Dock holds and releases money paid for provider bookings (venues, catering, entertainment, equipment and other services) made through Gala Dock. They form part of the Customer Terms, Organizer Terms, Provider Marketplace Terms and Venue Marketplace Terms.

## 1. How the money flows

1.1 When a customer or organiser pays for a booking (deposit, balance, emergency booking or standby fee), the payment is taken by **Gala Dock as commercial agent for the provider**, through our payment processor, Stripe. Gala Dock is the merchant of record for that payment.

1.2 The payment is then **held** in Gala Dock's Stripe platform balance. The booking shows **"Paid · protected until delivery"**.

1.3 Gala Dock releases the provider's share (the booking amount less Gala Dock's commission) by transferring it to the provider's connected Stripe account. The commission rate is the one recorded on the booking when it was paid. Later changes to plans or rates don't apply to it.

1.4 Payment is treated as received by the provider when the customer pays Gala Dock. This means the customer's obligation to pay is discharged by paying Gala Dock, even before the money is released to the provider.

## 2. When money is released

The provider's share becomes **eligible for release** when **all** of the following apply:

* **Delivery is evidenced:** the booking has been marked fulfilled or completed, **or** 48 hours have passed since the event ended while the booking is still confirmed or active. Standby fees are an exception: they are earned when the standby is accepted.
* There is **no open dispute** about the booking.
* There is **no unresolved chargeback** on the payment.
* There is **no cancellation awaiting a decision**.

The transfer is then made automatically, provided the provider's payout account is active and in good standing with Stripe. If it isn't, the money stays protected and the provider is asked to finish payout setup. Release can also depend on Stripe settling the original card payment.

## 3. Disputes

3.1 Either side can open a dispute from the booking. Customers should do this within **48 hours after the event ends**, before release.

3.2 Both sides can add a statement and evidence (photos, documents, messages). Messages exchanged through Gala Dock form part of the record.

3.3 A Gala Dock reviewer decides on the basis of the booking terms, the provider's policy, the evidence, and what is fair and reasonable. Possible outcomes:
* release in full;
* a partial refund to the customer with the rest released to the provider;
* a full refund.

Every decision records written reasons, which both sides can see.

3.4 Our decision settles how the money held by Gala Dock is distributed. It is not a court judgment: either party keeps any right to bring a claim against the other, and consumers keep their statutory rights.

## 4. Refunds after release

If a refund is due after some or all of the provider's share has been released (for example a later cancellation or a chargeback the customer wins), we reverse the provider's share in proportion from its connected account or recover it under the Provider Marketplace Terms.

## 5. Chargebacks

If the card holder raises a chargeback, the payment stays protected until the card network decides. We submit the booking evidence. If the chargeback is lost, the amount is recovered as described in section 4, unless we decide the loss was caused by our own error.

## 6. Limits

6.1 Protection applies only to payments made **through Gala Dock**. Payments made directly to a provider outside the platform are not protected, and asking for off-platform payment breaks our standards.

6.2 Payment protection is not insurance. It doesn't cover consequential losses (for example travel costs or lost earnings), and it doesn't replace event insurance.

6.3 Protection depends on our processor. If Stripe holds or reverses funds under its own rules, we will tell the affected parties and handle the booking fairly.

## 7. What the screens say

| State shown | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Amount due | Not yet paid |
| Paid · protected until delivery | Payment received and held |
| Provider payout pending | Eligible for release; transfer in progress |
| Released to provider | Transferred to the provider's account |
| Refunded / Partially refunded | Money returned to the payer |
| Disputed | On hold until the dispute or chargeback is decided |
