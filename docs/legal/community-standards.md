# Community & Marketplace Standards

> **Draft for specialist UK legal review before production publication.** Version 2026-10.

Gala Dock works when customers can trust listings and reviews, and providers and organisers can trust that everyone plays fair. These standards explain what we expect and how we enforce it. They apply alongside the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 1. Honest listings

* Show what you actually offer. Use real photos of your venue, food, setup or performances. Stock or illustrative images must be labelled.
* Prices must include every compulsory charge. Optional extras are fine if they're clearly optional.
* Capacities must be safe and within licence.
* Badges, awards and certifications must be real and current.
* Availability must be kept up to date. Repeatedly accepting requests you can't fulfil damages your reliability score and may lead to restrictions.

## 2. Reliable conduct

* Respond to requests within the stated window, or decline promptly.
* Honour quotes, holds and standby commitments.
* Communicate through Gala Dock, so both parties have a record and payment protection applies.
* Turn up, deliver what was agreed, and tell the other party early if something changes.

Your **reliability score** reflects response time, acceptance, on-time delivery, cancellations and disputes. It is calculated automatically, explained in your workspace, and affects your placement in search. We don't sell placement inside organic results. Paid promotions are labelled "Sponsored".

## 3. Reviews

* Only customers with a completed booking or attended event can review it.
* Reviews must describe a genuine experience. No fake, paid, incentivised or coordinated reviews, and no reviewing yourself or your competitors.
* Businesses can respond publicly once, politely, and without revealing personal data.
* We remove reviews that are unlawful, abusive, irrelevant, contain personal data, or are fake. We don't remove reviews simply because they are negative. Removal decisions record a reason and can be appealed.
* Average ratings are calculated from all published reviews. We don't suppress or reorder reviews to favour paying customers.

## 4. Respectful communication

No harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual content or pressure tactics in messages, listings or reviews. Report anything that worries you.

## 5. Templates and community content

Public templates must follow the Content & Template Policy. Templates with three or more independent reports are hidden automatically while we review them.

## 6. Enforcement ladder

1. **Guidance:** a notice explaining the issue.
2. **Content action:** editing a listing (with notice), or removing a review or template.
3. **Restriction:** limits on publishing, messaging, resale, promotions, template publishing or payouts.
4. **Suspension:** temporary loss of access while we investigate a serious issue.
5. **Termination:** for serious or repeated breaches, fraud or safety risks.

Serious harm can lead straight to suspension. We tell you what we decided and why, unless doing so would prejudice an investigation or the law prevents it, and you can appeal through the Complaints Policy.
